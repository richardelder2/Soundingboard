# The Bracket Method: Developmental Editing

The **Bracket Method** is a surgical developmental editing technique designed for prose scenes. It enforces strict cause-and-effect progression and psychological realism while protecting the author's authentic voice from being overwritten by editorial summary or generic AI prose.

---

## 1. Core Principles

1. **Preserve Prose Verbatim:** Good prose is quoted in plain text. An editor's duty is to fix architecture and sequencing, not homogenize the author's stylistic fingerprint.
2. **Brackets Enclose Editorial Voice:** Every editorial instruction, cut justification, reorder note, and prose suggestion is strictly bracketed: `[like this]`.
3. **Swain Motivating-Reaction Units (MRUs):** Every micro-beat inside a scene must follow physical stimulus and psychological reaction in natural human order:
   $$\text{Stimulus / Action} \longrightarrow \text{Sensation} \longrightarrow \text{Emotion} \longrightarrow \text{Thought} \longrightarrow \text{Irrevocable Action}$$
   *Rule:* A character must never register emotional grief before the sensory discovery of tragedy, nor relief before danger has physically passed.

---

## 2. The Bracket Taxonomy

*   `[PRESERVE]` — Affirms strong, evocative prose. Briefly explains the sensory or dramatic mechanism that makes it work so the writer internalizes the success.
*   `[CUT]` — Marks prose for deletion. Used when sentences tell what has already been shown, pre-empt an emotional payoff, or dilute momentum with repetitive throat-clearing.
*   `[REORDER]` — Flags prose that is narratively effective but positioned incorrectly in the cause-and-effect chain. Specifies the exact landing spot in the revised sequence.
*   `[STRUCTURAL NOTE: ...]` — Illuminates the narrative physics behind a requested change: missing progressive complications, unearned value shifts, or broken stimulus-response chains.
*   `[DRAFT SUGGESTION: ...]` — Supplies a rough bridging draft for a missing beat, tailored to the scene's voice anchor. Always explicitly framed as an editable prompt for the author.

---

## 3. The 3-Step Execution Protocol

1. **Macro Diagnostic:** Isolate the turning point. Determine whether the scene turns on an **Action** (physical event or irreversible deed) or a **Revelation** (new information that upends the character's worldview).
2. **Causal Chain Audit:** Trace every sentence against its immediate predecessor. Replace episodic transitions (*"and then"*) with causal transitions (*"therefore"* or *"but"*).
3. **Chronological Revision Pass:** Re-assemble the scene into its ideal emotional trajectory, embedding bracket tags directly alongside the author's original sentences. Conclude with a bulleted rewrite summary.
