# Spatial Transit Calculus & Anti-Teleport Discipline

In speculative fiction, historical drama, and epic adventures (*The Lord of the Rings*, *Master and Commander*, *Dune*, *True Grit*), geography is not passive scenery; **geography is a relentless mathematical resistance machine**.

The single fastest way an author shatters reader immersion is the **"Teleporting Character" defect**: having a messenger or army cross 250 miles of mountain wasteland in two days without explaining how the laws of physics were suspended.

---

## 1. Physical Velocity Benchmarks

When calculating how long an overland or sea journey takes, rely on historical logistics:

| Mode of Travel | Realistic Daily Pace | Limiting Friction |
|---|---|---|
| **Infantry / Common March** | 15–20 miles / day | Blistered feet, heavy packs, supply carts (wagons slow armies to 10–12 mi/day). |
| **Mounted Travel (Sustained)**| 25–35 miles / day | Horses cannot gallop for hours. They walk and trot; they require grain, water, and rest. |
| **Imperial Relay Courier** | 60–80 miles / day | Requires pre-established post-stations with fresh remount horses every 15–20 miles. |
| **River Barge (Downstream)** | 25–35 miles / day | Rapid with current; upstream drops to 6–8 miles/day via grueling towlines or poles. |
| **Ocean Sailing (Favorable)** | 75–120 miles / day | Subject to calms, adverse headwinds, squalls, navigation errors, and hull fouling. |
| **Sublight / Sci-Fi STL** | Weeks to Months | Fuel mass fractions, acceleration G-forces, heat dissipation limits. |

---

## 2. The Three Forces of Logistical Attrition

1. **The Forage Radius:** An army or party cannot carry infinite food. Beyond 10 days of travel, wagons are carrying the food required to feed the draught animals pulling the wagons.
2. **Terrain Chokepoints:** Swollen spring rivers have only one ford for 50 miles; mountain passes close from early snow; swamps turn 5 miles into a grueling two-day slog.
3. **Information Speed vs. Physical Speed:** Before the telegraph or magical communication, news traveled at the speed of a galloping horse or a carrier bird. If the capital is 500 miles away, the King cannot know of a border rebellion until two weeks after it begins.

---

## 3. Collaborative Revision Pass (Spatial Integrity)

When checking scene travel logic:
- **Option A (The Pacing Compression Pass):** Keep the travel time realistic, but compress the journey through narrative summary: *"Six days of rain and muddy shale brought them to the foothills of Ironspire."*
- **Option B (The Environmental Crisis):** Exploit the journey's duration to stage a dramatic turning point (exhaustion confession, supply sabotage, ambush at a river ford).
- **Option C (The Plausible Exception):** If characters must arrive overnight, introduce an expensive, high-risk exception (killing their horses in a desperate relay ride, dangerous tidal current, illicit portal).
