## 1. Professional Query Letter (The Standard 1-Page Pitch)

**Dear [AGENT_NAME / EDITORIAL_TEAM],**

[PERSONALIZED HOOK / CONNECTION: *I am seeking representation for [TITLE], a [WORD_COUNT]-word [GENRE] novel that will appeal to fans of [COMP_TITLE_1] by [AUTHOR_1] and [COMP_TITLE_2] by [AUTHOR_2]. I noticed your manuscript wishlist specifically called out [ELEMENT_THEY_WANT, e.g. atmospheric political intrigue / morally gray protagonists / locked-room sci-fi].*]

[PARAGRAPH 1 - THE PROTAGONIST & INCITING INCIDENT: *Introduce protagonist, their station, their core inner desire/fear, and the inciting event that shatters their equilibrium.*]

[PARAGRAPH 2 - THE ESCALATING CONFLICT & OPPOSITION: *Detail the complications. Who or what opposes them? What trap or conspiracy does the protagonist stumble into? Show the rising stakes and ticking clock.*]

[PARAGRAPH 3 - THE CRISIS / BEST BAD CHOICE: *Bring the query to its climax. What impossible choice must the protagonist make? What catastrophic loss awaits failure? Do not summarize the entire ending here; leave the agent clamoring for the manuscript.*]

[BIO / AUTHOR CREDENTIALS: *About myself: [Brief bio, relevant writing background, publications, awards, or domain expertise that informs the story's authenticity]. The complete manuscript is finished and available upon request.*]

**Thank you for your time and consideration,**

[AUTHOR_NAME]  
[EMAIL / CONTACT]

## 2. Standard 1-Page Narrative Synopsis (500–800 words)
*A transparent, comprehensive summary covering the full plot arc, including the midpoint turn, dark night of the soul, climax, and exact resolution.*

### Act 1: Setup & Inciting Incident
[Detail the opening world, character wounds, inciting incident, and the First Plot Point decision where the protagonist crosses into the Special World.]

### Act 2A: Rising Action & Midpoint Shift
[Outline the early trials, new allies/rivals, and the critical Midpoint Shift where the protagonist shifts from reactive to proactive, raising the central conflict from personal to universal.]

### Act 2B: Crisis & The All Hope Is Lost Climax
[The antagonist strikes back. False victory or devastating defeat. The Dark Night of the Soul where the protagonist must surrender their lie to embrace truth.]

### Act 3: Climax & Resolution
[The final showdown or test. Exactly how the protagonist wins or loses, what sacrifices are made, and the final state of the world and character arc (no cliffhangers allowed in agent synopses).]
