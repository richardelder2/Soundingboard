# Thematic Resonance & Motif Weaver Playbook

## When this applies
The author wants to deepen the thematic resonance of a scene, embed subtle physical motifs, or avoid preachy exposition. Common triggers:
- *"How do I highlight the theme of [Theme, e.g. Decay / Complicity] in this scene?"*
- *"Weave thematic subtext into this chapter without preaching."*
- *"The scene feels thematically hollow; give me physical motifs."*
- *"Help me show the theme through action rather than narrator explanation."*

## What good output looks like
- **STRICT CRAFT RULE: The Narrator Never States the Theme.**
  Theme must NEVER be announced via narrator summation, philosophical inner monologues, or moralizing wrap-ups. It must arise mechanically from:
  1. *Environmental Symbolism:* Physical objects, decay/growth, architecture, light, weather.
  2. *Character Actions & Mechanical Habits:* Repetitive actions, fidgets, ways characters touch or discard objects.
  3. *Dialogue Subtext:* Conversations where characters argue about something small that secretly mirrors the moral dilemma.
- **Sample Blended Passage:** Provide a short draft excerpt demonstrating how environmental symbols and behavioral motifs interact seamlessly in prose.

## Context
Execute the mechanical context packer:
```bash
node scripts/pack-theme-weaver.js [chapter_or_theme]
# or:
node scripts/soundingboard.js pack theme [chapter_or_theme]
```
Use the output (`FOOLSCAP CONTROLLING IDEA`, `STRUCTURE PLAN THEME DIALS`, `TARGET CHAPTER EXCERPT`, `AUTHENTICITY RULE 4`) to anchor your suggestions.

## Process
1. **Define the Thematic Axis:**
   - Identify the controlling idea (e.g. "Sacrifice leads to liberation, but only when voluntary").
2. **Translate Abstract Theme into Tangible Matter:**
   - Brainstorm 3 physical manifestations:
     - Environmental: How does the room/setting physically reflect the theme?
     - Behavioral: What small, non-obvious action does the character perform that externalizes their internal moral state?
     - Conversational: What mundane topic becomes a proxy for the thematic clash?
3. **Draft Integrated Prose Demonstration:**
   - Write 1–2 paragraphs blending these elements without ever naming the thematic concept directly.

## Output format

```markdown
### 🪢 Thematic Resonance Strategy: [Theme Topic]

#### 1. 🏛️ Environmental Symbolism (Setting as Mirror)
- **Symbolic Element:** [Physical object, weather shift, or architectural detail]
- **Dramatic Role:** [How it reflects the theme passively in the background]

#### 2. 🤲 Character Behavior & Physical Motifs (Action as Subtext)
- **Motif / Habit:** [Specific small physical motion, e.g. constantly straightening a frayed cuff, polishing a tarnished tool]
- **Subconscious Meaning:** [What emotional truth the character is trying to suppress]

#### 3. 💬 Dialogue Subtext (The Mundane Proxy)
- **Overt Conversation:** [What they are talking about on the surface, e.g. the price of bread, a leaking roof]
- **Thematic Undercurrent:** [The real moral reckoning being negotiated]

---

### ✍️ Blended Demonstration Excerpt
> [1–2 paragraphs of drafted prose demonstrating the environment, action, and dialogue working together without any narrator explanation.]

---

*Would you like to weave these motifs into your active chapter beats or draft?*
```
