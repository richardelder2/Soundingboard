# Investigation & Forensic Evidence Chain Matrix

*In legal thrillers, police procedurals, hardboiled noir, and forensic mysteries, evidence cannot merely appear—it must follow an unbroken **chain of custody, statutory admissibility rules, and forensic logic**. This matrix tracks physical exhibits, digital footprints, witness statements, and evidentiary vulnerabilities across the trial or investigation.*

| Motion Type | Moving Party | Statutory Grounds / Rule | Key Precedent or Argument | Judge's Ruling | Turning Point Consequence |
|:------------|:------------:|:-------------------------|:--------------------------|:--------------:|:--------------------------|
| **Motion in Limine** | Defense | Fourth Amendment violation (unreasonable search) | Warrant overreach | Denied in part | Defense must fight at trial |
| **Motion for Directed Verdict** | Defense | Failure of prosecution to establish prima facie intent | Insufficient evidence post-state's case | Denied (Standard practice) | Puts defendant on stand |
