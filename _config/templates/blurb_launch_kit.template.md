## 3. Full Commercial Back-Cover Blurb (150–220 words)
*The canonical 3-paragraph commercial back cover formula (Character & World -> The Catalyst & Escalation -> The Impossible Stakes / Best Bad Choice).*

### Paragraph 1: The Status Quo & The Hunger (Character & World)
[Introduce protagonist, their defining skill/wound, and what they stand to lose. Ground the tone, viscosity, and rules of the world in the very first sentence.]

### Paragraph 2: The Catalyst & The Web (The Disruption)
[The inciting incident strikes. An unexpected discovery, betrayal, or assignment pulls the protagonist out of safety and into unfamiliar, hostile territory. Antagonistic forces or ticking clocks close in.]

### Paragraph 3: The Crux & The Impossible Choice (The Stakes)
[Frame the climax without spoiling the ending. The protagonist faces their Best Bad Choice: compromise their deepest vow, or watch everything burn. Conclude with a punchy standalone thematic kicker.]
