## 2. The Asymmetric Collision Web (Dependencies & Debts)

*Factions do not exist in isolation. Map the active friction points between power blocks:*

- **F-01 (Iron Guild) ➔ F-02 (Holy Synod):**
  - *Dynamic:* Symbiotic Hostility.
  - *Leverage:* Synod needs Iron Guild weapons for their crusades; Guild pays heavy indulgences to keep inquisitors out of their accounting houses.
- **F-02 (Holy Synod) ➔ F-03 (River Barons):**
  - *Dynamic:* Imperial Cold War.
  - *Leverage:* River Barons tax church grain barges; Synod threatens to excommunicate the Barons' flagship captains.
- **F-03 (River Barons) ➔ F-04 (Night Syndicate):**
  - *Dynamic:* Parasitic Collusion.
  - *Leverage:* Barons take 20% kickbacks on smuggled goods; Syndicate holds evidence that would hang three Barons if leaked.
- **F-04 (Night Syndicate) ➔ F-01 (Iron Guild):**
  - *Dynamic:* Supply Chain Sabotage.
  - *Leverage:* Syndicate sells black-market ore that undercuts Guild fixed prices.
