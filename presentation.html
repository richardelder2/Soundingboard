08 · Editorial Integrity

The Bracket Method &
The Human Red Pen.

Playbooks #18 & #20: Eliminating black-box rewrites forever through quarantined, collaborative choice.

The 4-Phase Diagnostic Protocol
Phase 1
Objective Diagnosis: Zero text alterations. Mechanical scans flag cadence monotony, AI tells (>0.50/1k words), or POV voice drift.
Phase 2
Quarantined Brackets: Suggestions are isolated in brackets [AI-TELL: ...] alongside verbatim author prose.
Phase 3
The 3 Author Choices: Clear options: (1) Cut the friction, (2) Rephrase with 2–3 voice options, or (3) Keep as author voice.
Phase 4
Author-Executed Revision: Author makes the decision. The AI never unilaterally applies silent edits.
[PLAYBOOK #18: THE BRACKET METHOD SIMULATOR]
Diagnostic Finding: Explained Theme / Cliché Marker:
"She realized hope was a fragile bird in the winter dusk."
Choose Author Action:
> SCRIPT [CUT]: Remove summary sentence entirely. End scene on the cold click of the deadbolt.
