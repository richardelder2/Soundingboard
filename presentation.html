07 · Editorial Integrity

The Human-in-the-Loop Revision Playbook.

Why autonomous AI editing ruins voice—and how Soundingboard guarantees the author always holds the red pen.

The 4-Phase HITL Revision Cycle
Phase 1
Objective Diagnosis: Zero text modifications. Mechanical scans flag cadence spikes, AI tells (>0.50/1k words), or canon contradictions.
Phase 2
The 3-Option Fork: Agent presents distinct craft choices (The Cut, Dramatize, Subtext Shift, or Custom Direction).
Phase 3
Author Sign-Off: Author picks their favored option in chat. Zero prose changes occur until the author says "Proceed."
Phase 4
Surgical Execution: Minimal, targeted revision applied, followed by machine re-audit before gate clearance.
