# Heist & Caper Mechanics Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE IMPOSSIBLE TARGET & ARCHITECTURAL VAULT (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the heist planning table. Ask if they are ready to assemble a crew of specialists to steal something impossible from someone who deserves to lose it.
2. "What is the Target / Prize? (e.g., an uninsurable royal emerald, a ledger proving financial treason, an encrypted cold-storage crypto wallet inside a subterranean casino, or an ancient grimoire in a private museum?)."
3. "What is the Fortification / Fortress? Describe the physical and technological security layers of the location (e.g., biometric floor sensors, retinal scanners, private mercenary patrols, 12-ton bank vault doors, airtight vacuum seals, laser grids)."
4. "What is the Public Event or Gala providing cover? (Every great heist needs a gala, an auction, a festival, or an eclipse where wealthy targets are distracted in evening wear while the heist occurs underneath)."

## STAGE 2: THE SCORE, THE MOTIVE & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *One Last Job + Uncrackable Casino Vault + Grudge Against the House*, or *Found Family Rogues + High-Tech Museum Gala + Double-Cross Payoff*). What score are we planning?"
6. "What is the Mastermind's personal stake? Why isn't this job just about money? (e.g., avenging a betrayed mentor, clearing a family debt, or taking down an untouchable oligarch who destroyed their life)."
7. "The 'Clean Hands' Code: What is the crew's ethical operating line? (e.g., 'nobody gets hurt,' 'we only steal from monsters,' or 'we leave the guards tied up with a box of donuts')."

## STAGE 3: THE SPECIALIST CREW & CREW CHEMISTRY
8. "Assemble the Crew of 4–5 Specialists. Who fills each critical role?
   - **The Mastermind / Grifter:** (Face, charisma, social engineering, timing maestro)
   - **The Safecracker / Infiltrator:** (Acrobatics, tactile vault manipulation, laser-grid timing)
   - **The Hacker / Tech Specialist:** (Feed looping, comms jamming, digital backdoors)
   - **The Muscle / Distraction Artist:** (Explosives, tactical exits, bar fights on cue)
   - **The Inside Asset / Inside Woman:** (Underpaid employee, disillusioned secretary, or coerced bodyguard)."
9. "What is the simmering interpersonal friction inside the crew? (e.g., two ex-lovers who haven't worked together in three years, or the paranoid veteran forced to train the hotheaded rookie prodigy)."
10. "What is the 'dry run' or minor practice heist in Act 1 that demonstrates the crew's chemistry and establishes their operational flaws?"

## STAGE 4: THE OPPOSITION & THE OBSESSION
11. "Who is the Target / Mark? What makes this villain so detestable that the audience will cheer when they are stripped of their wealth and pride?"
12. "Who is the Security Architect / Investigator? (e.g., the obsessive casino security chief who never leaves the camera room, or the relentless Interpol inspector who recognizes the Mastermind's signature style)."
13. "What unexpected wrench gets thrown into the security system 48 hours before the job? (e.g., the museum upgrades to thermal cameras, or the Mark changes the vault combination daily at midnight)."

## STAGE 5: THE EXECUTION, THE BREAKDOWN & THE DOUBLE-BLUFF
14. "The Five Phases of the Job: How does each phase click into place on the night of the score?
    - Phase 1: Infiltration & Cover Story
    - Phase 2: Security System Jamming / Blackout
    - Phase 3: Vault Breach & Asset Acquisition
    - Phase 4: The Inevitable Complication / Alarm Trip
    - Phase 5: The Egress & Vanishing Act"
15. "The Crisis / Apparent Failure (75-80%): What goes catastrophically wrong? When does it look like the crew is caught red-handed, an ally betrays them, or the vault is completely empty?"
16. "The Ocean's Double-Bluff (The Twist): How does the story reveal that the 'catastrophe' was actually Part of the Mastermind's Real Plan all along? What did the reader see happening in plain sight that was completely misinterpreted?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What narrative structure best fits the caper? (Linear chronometer with ticking timestamps; or a non-linear structure using clever flashbacks to reveal hidden crew preparations?)."
18. "How should the dialogue and banter register? (Fast-paced, witty, overlapping dialogue with status-play reversals and slick verbal timing)."
19. "The Getaway & the Split: Where does the crew end up? How do they celebrate, where is the treasure stashed, and what hook hints at the next legendary mark?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an irresistible, razor-sharp heist blueprint with precision timing, charismatic specialists, escalating security obstacles, and a brilliant double-bluff climax. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a high-energy commercial blurb hook.
