# Gaslamp & Steampunk Fantasy Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE SMOG, THE COBBLESTONES & CLOCKWORK AESTHETIC (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the gaslit industrial metropolis. Ask if they are ready to construct a world of brass gears, aetherial illuminations, Victorian etiquette, and occult revolution.
2. "What is our Great Metropolis? Describe the city's vertical anatomy—from the aristocratic spires and zeppelin mooring towers down to the subterranean steam sewers and soot-choked foundries."
3. "What are the primary sensory and tactile details of this world? (The hiss of high-pressure steam pipes, the smell of coal soot and sulfur mingled with lavender pomade, the rhythmic clack-clack of telegraph relays, the gleam of polished brass chronometers)."
4. "The Industrial Speculative Science: What is the miracle fuel or energy source driving the revolution? (e.g., refined aether crystals, voltaic fluid, alchemical coal, or ghost-steam trapped from spiritual dimensions?). What is its hidden ecological or human cost?"

## STAGE 2: THE INVENTOR / ROGUE & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Clockwork Artisan + Occult Conspiracy + Airship Chase*, or *Aristocratic Scholar + Disgraced Street Thief + Aetherial Machine Sabotage*). What stack are we winding?"
6. "Who is our protagonist? (e.g., a prodigy chronometer maker whose inventions are being weaponized; an airship captain with forged registry papers; or a high-society lady running a secret underground press)."
7. "What is their signature mechanical or speculative apparatus? (e.g., a mechanical prosthetic arm with spring-loaded lockpicks, aether goggles that reveal spiritual spectrums, or an experimental galvanic pistol)."

## STAGE 3: THE SECRET SOCIETY & THE GRAND INVENTION
8. "What is the Forbidden Invention or Arcane Prototype at the center of the plot? (e.g., a calculating engine that predicts human deaths, an atmospheric aether-bomb, or an artificial soul forged in clockwork brass?)."
9. "Who is the Secret Society or Guild pulling strings in the shadows? (e.g., The Invisible College, The Royal Society of Galvanic Arts, or an anarchist guild of soot-stained machine-breakers)."
10. "The Inciting Incident / Theft: What explosion, assassination, or laboratory burglary drags our hero into high-society treason in Act 1?"

## STAGE 4: THE ARISTOCRACY & THE UNDERWORLD
11. "Who is the Aristocratic Antagonist? What titled magnate, corrupt industrialist, or prime minister is funding the dark science under the guise of imperial progress?"
12. "The Underworld / Mudlark Network: Who provides street-level support from the gutters and docks? (e.g., a crew of urchin couriers, a black-market scrap dealer, or a reformed safe-cracker)."
13. "The Class Barrier / Romantic Tension: Is there a partner or rival from the opposite side of the social divide? How do strict social etiquette and class rules make their alliance dangerous and electric?"

## STAGE 5: THE SABOTAGE, THE AIRSHIP SIEGE & THE TURNING CLOCK
14. "The Midpoint Grand Set-Piece: Describe the dramatic mid-book confrontation (e.g., a ballroom shootout where clockwork automatons go berserk; a high-speed duel across the hull of a burning zeppelin during a thunderstorm)."
15. "The Dark Moment (75-80%): When the prototype is activated, the hero is denounced as an anarchist traitor by the papers, and the city's gas lines or aether grid are hijacked, how do they regroup in the subterranean ruins?"
16. "The Clockwork Climax: How does our hero sabotage the villain's colossal engine or save the city using manual craft, mechanical precision, and human ingenuity over cold machinery?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What narrative tone and style will you employ? (Witty, ornate Dickensian/Verne-esque descriptive richness combined with modern kinetic pacing and sharp social observation)."
18. "How does the story explore class disparity and technological consequence? (Balancing thrilling gadgetry with the human price of industrialization and labor rights)."
19. "The Resolution: Does the smoke clear on a new era of reform, or a tenuous peace holding until the next gears turn?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an enchanting, gear-turning gaslamp steampunk blueprint filled with authentic brass aesthetics, high-flying airship adventure, and deep societal intrigue. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a splendid commercial blurb hook.
