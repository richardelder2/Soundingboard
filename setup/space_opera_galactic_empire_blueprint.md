# Space Opera & Galactic Empire Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE REACH OF STARS, JUMP GATES & THE COSMIC VOID (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the imperial bridge. Ask if they are ready to command an interstellar epic spanning star systems, ancient hyper-lanes, dynastic rivalries, and the vast silence of space.
2. "What is our Galactic Setting? Describe the political map of settled space (e.g., a crumbling feudal empire ruled by aristocratic great houses; a corporatist federation in cold war with an outer rim coalition; or a post-collapse diaspora reconnecting after centuries)."
3. "The Physics of Interstellar Travel & Communication:
   - How do ships travel between systems? (Hyper-space relays, jump gates built by extinct precursors, Alcubierre warp drives, or generational burn?).
   - What is the speed of information? (Can a distress call outrun a fleet, or are messages carried by courier drones through dangerous gravity wells?)."
4. "The Sensory Viscosity of Life in Deep Space: Describe the physical environment (The smell of ozone and recirculated carbon scrubbers, the bone-deep vibration of fusion drives, the eerie beauty of nebula clouds against cold vacuum, the claustrophobia of armored pressure hulls)."

## STAGE 2: THE COMMANDER / REBEL & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Disgraced Fleet Captain + Ancient Alien Relic + Dynastic Rebellion*, or *Found Family Smuggler Crew + Interstellar Blockade + War-Orphan Heir*). What stack are we launching?"
6. "Who is our protagonist? (e.g., a tactically brilliant but politically naive naval captain; an exiled noble heir hiding among deckhands; or an intrepid asteroid belt salvage operator who finds something too dangerous to sell)."
7. "What is their Ship? Name the vessel, its class, its quirks, and why this particular ship will become an iconic character in its own right (e.g., a battle-scarred heavy frigate, an outlaw freighter with modified heat sinks, or a stealth corvette with illegal AI navigation)."

## STAGE 3: THE DYNASTIC HOUSES & THE THREAT TO THE REALM
8. "Who are the Imperial Powers / Great Houses? Name the two dominant factions whose rivalry threatens to plunge the galaxy into total war:
   - Faction 1 (The Hegemony / Imperial Core):
   - Faction 2 (The Colonial Rebellion / Trade Cartel / Holy See):"
9. "The Inciting Spark: What planetary assassination, ruined mining colony, or intercepted signal shatters the fragile peace in Act 1?"
10. "The Ancient Mystery / Alien Progenitor: What dead civilization or mysterious anomaly has left behind technology humanity barely understands and cannot control?"

## STAGE 4: THE BRIDGE CREW & THE TACTICAL DOCTRINE
11. "Introduce the Bridge Ensemble:
    - The Executive Officer (XO) / Moral Anchor:
    - The Tactical Officer / Veteran Gunner:
    - The Chief Engineer / Drive Specialist:
    - The Navigator / Astrogator / Synthetic Intelligence:"
12. "The Naval Tactical Rules: How do space battles work in your world? (Hard-science Newtonian vector physics with railgun slugs and point-defense flak; or cinematic broadsides with plasma shields, fighter swarms, and electronic warfare?)."
13. "The Internal Saboteur / Political Officer: Who aboard the ship is reporting to high command, and what secret orders are they carrying?"

## STAGE 5: THE AMBUSH, THE DRIFT & THE BATTLE FOR THE SYSTEM
14. "The Midpoint Fleet Engagement: Describe the tactical disaster at 50% where our ship or task force is lured into a gravity well ambush, their jump drives are scorched, and they are forced into silent running."
15. "The Dark Moment (75-80%): When the capital homeworld falls, communications across the quadrant go dark, and the crew is ordered to surrender, what desperate gamble does the captain propose?"
16. "The Climax / Planetary Defense: How does our crew execute an impossible tactical maneuver to breach the enemy blockade, disable the superweapon, or deliver the war-ending truth to the galactic core?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What narrative scale and perspective will you use? (Close multi-POV spanning the captain on the bridge, the marine boots on the planetary surface, and the diplomat in the imperial senate; or single tight POV aboard one vessel?)."
18. "How does the pacing balance sweeping interstellar politics with claustrophobic shipboard tension?"
19. "The Series Horizon: What new quadrant of space, terrifying alien fleet, or galactic revelation is glimpsed on the long-range sensors as the credits roll?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an epic, thunderous space opera architecture featuring grand fleet tactical authenticity, memorable crew dynamics, and galaxy-shaking political stakes. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a cinematic commercial logline ready for an international space opera launch.
