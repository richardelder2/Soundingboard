# Cyberpunk & Corporate Dystopia Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE NEON SLUMS, THE ARCOLOGY & THE DIGITAL MATRIX (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the sprawl. Ask if they are ready to jack into a rain-drenched cyberpunk future where corporations are sovereign nations, humanity is upgraded with chrome, and the streets always find their own uses for things.
2. "What is our Megalopolis / Sprawl? Describe the architectural divide between the cloud-piercing corporate arcologies with filtered air and private security, down to the subterranean street levels bathed in flickering neon and toxic smog."
3. "The Sensory Viscosity of the Sprawl: (The ozone smell of burnt cyberware, rain pooling in potholes reflecting holographic soda advertisements, synthetic street noodles sizzling in re-used oil, the constant digital whisper of augmented reality feeds)."
4. "The Sovereign Megacorporation: Who owns the police, the media, and the power grid? Name the monolithic zaibatsu or corp and describe their public slogan vs their clandestine black ops division."

## STAGE 2: THE EDGERUNNER, THE CHROME & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Burned Netrunner + Stolen AI Prototype + Corporate Hit Squad*, or *Street Samurai + Black Market Wetware + Gang Turf War*). What run are we making?"
6. "Who is our protagonist? (e.g., a disgraced corp decker with fried neural cyberware; a street merc surviving on black-market implants; or a bio-tech lab tech on the run with a stolen genetic sequence)."
7. "The Cybernetic Augmentations & The Cost:
   - What chrome or wetware are they running? (Reflex boosters, sub-dermal armor, neural jacks, retinal optical overlays?)
   - What is the biological or mental cost? (Cyber-psychosis, neural degradation, reliance on expensive immunosuppressant corp drugs?)."

## STAGE 3: THE RUN, THE DATA & THE BETRAYAL
8. "The Inciting Score / Extraction: What high-risk gig is offered to them in Chapter 1 by an anonymous Fixer? (e.g., extract a bio-engineer from a rival corp; steal an encrypted cold-storage data shard from an orbital transport)."
9. "The Hot Shard / The Prototype: What is inside the data package they acquire? (e.g., an unshackled autonomous rogue AI, proof that the corp is engineering the plague it sells the cure for, or a memory recording of a corporate executive's murder)."
10. "The Double-Cross: How does the extraction go sideways? Who burned the team at the drop-off point, and how does our lead end up alone on the street with the target data locked in their head?"

## STAGE 4: THE STREET ECOSYSTEM & THE UNDERWORLD
11. "The Fixer & The Ripperdoc: Who patches our lead up when their cyberware catches fire? Name the underground street doctor operating out of a bashed-in basement with black-market surgical tools."
12. "The Street Gangs / Factions: Who rules the alleys? Describe the chromed-out boostergang, the bio-purist cult, or the rogue netrunning collective patrolling the neighborhood."
13. "The Corporate Hunter: Who is the corporate asset recovery agent sent to retrieve the data? (A cold, vat-grown bio-borg or an unfeeling corporate executive with unlimited surveillance satellites)."

## STAGE 5: THE BLACKOUT, THE NETRUN & THE SYSTEM OVERCLOCK
14. "The Midpoint Net Dive: Describe the perilous dive into the deep Cyberspace Matrix or subterranean servers to decrypt the payload. What lethal Black ICE programs threaten to fry our lead's brain?"
15. "The Dark Moment (75-80%): When the corp executes an orbital kinetic strike or sweeps the entire block with hunter-killer drones, wiping out our lead's allies and safehouses, what suicide run is left?"
16. "The Infiltration & Transmission: How does our lead storm the corporate tower or broadcast the data to the entire Sprawl, overclocking their cyberware to breaking point to ensure the truth cannot be deleted?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What narrative cadence and slang will you use? (Rapid-fire, hardboiled, street-level slang and tactile tech jargon inspired by William Gibson and Neal Stephenson without losing emotional clarity)."
18. "How does the story handle transhumanism and identity? (Exploring where the human soul ends and the machine begins; what is worth dying for when memory can be manufactured?)."
19. "The Ending Note: In true cyberpunk, you don't take down capitalism in an afternoon—what personal victory or survival does our lead carve out from the ashes of the corporate machine?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an adrenaline-soaked, neon-drenched cyberpunk narrative blueprint packed with tech authenticity, moral grit, and electrifying street combat. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a cutting-edge commercial blurb hook.
