# Urban Fantasy & Paranormal Investigator Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE CITY, THE VEIL & SUPERNATURAL GEOGRAPHY (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the supernatural underworld. Ask if they are ready to construct a modern metropolis where ancient magic runs through subway tunnels, fae courts meet in speakeasies, and mortals walk blindly past monsters.
2. "What is our City, and what gives it supernatural resonance? (e.g., Chicago with its ley lines and winter winds; London with centuries of blood in the Thames mud; New Orleans with bayou spirits and French Quarter crypts; or Seattle with mist and moss-covered totems?)."
3. "The Masquerade / The Veil: How is magic kept secret from normal mortals? (e.g., a psychological glamour that makes humans rationalize fireballs as gas-line explosions, or an ancient treaty enforced by a ruthless supernatural council with lethal hit squads?)."
4. "The Sensory Clash of Modern & Mythic: Describe the aesthetic friction (e.g., an arcane circle drawn in chalk on concrete beneath buzzing neon, an ancient rune carved into the cylinder of a .44 Magnum, or a smartphone shielded against electromagnetic magical blowback)."

## STAGE 2: THE DETECTIVE, THE MAGIC & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Wizard PI + Paranormal Murder Mystery + Modern City Masquerade*, or *Necromancer Ex-Cop + Demon Drug Epidemic + Reluctant Werewolf Ally*). What stack are we brewing?"
6. "Who is our Paranormal Sleuth / Practitioner? (e.g., an unlicensed wizard operating out of the yellow pages, a psychic bound to a sassy ghost, or a half-demon bounty hunter who owes back taxes to the IRS)."
7. "The Cost & Limitations of Their Magic:
   - What happens physically or mentally when they cast? (Exhaustion, burns, loss of blood, technopathy blowing out fuses?)
   - What is their signature magical focus or gear? (A carved blasting rod, silver rings, a leather duster lined with protective wards?)"

## STAGE 3: THE PARANORMAL CASE & THE FIRST BODY
8. "The Inciting Case: Who hires them in Chapter 1? (e.g., the city police liaison needing an off-the-books consultation; a terrified mob boss whose enforcer was shredded by something not human; or a frantic fae noble whose child was swapped)."
9. "The Crime Scene & Occult Forensics: What bizarre supernatural signature is left at the first murder scene? (e.g., ectoplasmic residue, melted silver, a heart removed without a chest incision, or a curse pattern burned into the ceiling)."
10. "The Ticking Clock & Escalation: What will happen in 72 hours if this entity or cult isn't stopped? (e.g., a planetary alignment that will rip open the Veil on Halloween night, or a demonic plague released into the municipal water supply)."

## STAGE 4: THE COURT FACTIONS & THE ALLY NETWORK
11. "The Supernatural Power Players: Who controls the city's dark corners?
    - The Vampire Syndicate / Blood Court:
    - The Werewolf Pack / Street Turf:
    - The Fae / High Court Diplomats:
    - The Rogue Practitioners / Demonologists:"
12. "The Skeptical Mortal Ally: Who is the mundane human detective or medical examiner who suspects the truth, and how does our lead protect them from becoming monster food?"
13. "The Familiar / Snarky Companion: (e.g., a talking skull with vast occult knowledge, an enchanted gargoyle, or an intelligent cat with attitude)."

## STAGE 5: THE CORNERING, THE BREACH & THE SPELLFIRE
14. "The Midpoint Trap: Where does our investigator get ambushed by overwhelming supernatural force at 50%? (e.g., their warded apartment is breached, their magical focus is shattered, or they are framed for the murder by the High Council)."
15. "The Dark Moment (75-80%): Stripped of their gear, bleeding, and excommunicated by the supernatural authorities, what dangerous bargain with a higher power or forbidden entity are they forced to strike?"
16. "The Climax / Occult Showdown: Where does the final confrontation take place? Describe the spellfire, physical combat, and clever subversion of a magical rule that allows our underpowered hero to bring down an ancient apex predator."

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What narrative voice will carry the story? (Signature first-person snark, dry humor, high emotional stakes, and world-weary investigative observation in the classic Jim Butcher / Ben Aaronovitch vein)."
18. "How does the pacing balance investigation and action set-pieces? (Interspersing gritty clue gathering and magical ritual preparations with explosive, kinetic action sequences)."
19. "The Aftermath: How is the Masquerade repaired, what debt is left unpaid for Book 2, and what beer is cracked open to wash down the blood?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an addictive, punchy, and atmospheric urban fantasy architecture with rock-solid magical mechanics, witty dialogue, and rich series runway. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a commercial blurb hook ready to headline an urban fantasy shelf.
