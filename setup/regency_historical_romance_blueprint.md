# Regency & Historical Romance Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE TON, THE SEASON & BALLROOM SPLENDOR (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to Mayfair during the London Season. Ask if they are ready to choreograph a dazzling dance of high-society etiquette, scandalous whispers, candlelit ballrooms, and undeniable forbidden passion.
2. "What is our Historical Setting & Year? (e.g., Regency London in 1815 during the Season; a lavish estate in the Scottish Highlands; or Bath during the quiet winter months?)."
3. "The Sensory Glories & Strict Etiquette of Society: Describe the sensory details (The rustle of silk and satin skirts over crinoline, the scent of lavender waters and beeswax candles in chandeliers, champagne flutes clinking across marble terraces, the scratch of quill pens writing invitations)."
4. "The Social Law & The Danger of Ruin: What strict rule of etiquette or society scandal threatens our heroine's family? (e.g., an impending bankruptcy if she doesn't secure a wealthy peer; a family secret that could banish them from Almack's; or a scandalous gossip sheet publishing blinded blinds)."

## STAGE 2: THE COUPLE, THE CONFLICT & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Rake Duke + Wallflower Spinster + Fake Courtship Bargain*, or *Enemies to Lovers + Forced Carriage Ride + Long-Standing Family Feud*). What stack are we courting?"
6. "Who is our Heroine? What makes her unconventional in the eyes of the Ton? (e.g., a sharp-witted bluestocking with a passion for botany; an outspoken wallflower resigned to spinsterhood; or an impoverished curate's daughter thrust into the matrimonial market)."
7. "Who is our Hero? (e.g., the notoriously cold and rakish Duke who has sworn never to marry for love; a brooding, war-hardened Viscount returning from Waterloo; or an arrogant, reform-minded Earl)."

## STAGE 3: THE PROXIMITY TRAP & THE SOCIAL BARGAIN
8. "The Arrangement / Trap: What forces these two into unavoidable proximity for 60%+ of the Season? (e.g., a fake engagement to ward off matchmaking mothers; a joint guardianship of an orphaned ward; a snowbound country house party in Yorkshire; or a scandalous compromise witnessed in a dark garden terrace)."
9. "The Signature Ballroom Dance: In which ball or assembly do they share their first waltz? Describe the physical contact (gloved hands touching, the cadence of the orchestra, the feeling of every eye in the room watching their every breath)."
10. "The Snappy Banter & Status Play: What is their verbal fencing style? How do their witty retorts mask genuine emotional vulnerability and mutual admiration?"

## STAGE 4: THE RIVALS, CHAPERONES & GOSSIP MONGER
11. "The Society Chaperone / Formidable Matriarch: Who is the eagle-eyed aunt, dowager countess, or mother monitoring their every glance with smelling salts in hand?"
12. "The Suitor / Rival: Who is the respectable, boring suitor offering a safe, loveless marriage, and why is the heroine's family pressuring her to accept?"
13. "The Gossip Sheet: Who is the anonymous columnist or salon host whose daily printed broadsheet dictates social life and death across the West End?"

## STAGE 5: THE SCANDAL, THE REPUTATION CRISIS & THE GRAND GESTURE
14. "The Midpoint Compromise / Kiss: Where does the physical tension snap at 50%? (In the library during a masquerade, in a private carriage during a thunderstorm, or on a secluded walk in Hyde Park?). What immediate societal or emotional complication arises?"
15. "The Dark Moment (75-80%): What misunderstanding, old promise, or public revelation threatens absolute social ruin and separates them, forcing the heroine to pack her trunks for the countryside?"
16. "The Climax / The Grand Ballroom Declaration: How does the hero defy the Ton's hypocritical rules, interrupt a formal gathering, or ride through the rain to declare his unconditional devotion before the entire aristocracy?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What heat level and pacing will you establish? (Sweet and witty closed-door romance in the Georgette Heyer/Austen tradition; simmering sensual slow-burn; or passionate, explicit Regency romance with modern emotional agency?)."
18. "How does the story weave authentic period detail without slowing the romantic tension? (Carriage mechanics, clothing rituals, cards of address, calling hours)."
19. "The Epilogue & Guaranteed HEA: Describe the domestic bliss, the wedding, or the quiet country terrace scene that rewards the readers' emotional investment."

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an irresistible, witty, and deeply romantic Regency blueprint filled with high-society tension, sparkling dialogue, and emotional resonance. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a breathless commercial blurb hook.
