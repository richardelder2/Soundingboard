# Espionage & Black Ops Action Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE GEOPOLITICAL SHADOWS & TRADE-CRAFT VISCOSITY (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the intelligence operations brief. Ask if they are ready to construct a high-octane tactical thriller where loyalties are transactional, every device is compromised, and the clock is counting down to zero.
2. "What is the primary operational theater and geopolitical friction point? (e.g., a rain-soaked diplomatic summit in Geneva, the lawless borderlands of the Levant, a sub-zero listening post in the Arctic Circle, or a deep-cover financial network in London?)."
3. "What are the authentic tactile details of modern clandestine tradecraft here? (Dead drops disguised as rusted infrastructure, burner phones scrubbed with Faraday pouches, passports bearing three distinct identities, the scent of damp asphalt and cordite)."
4. "What is the geopolitical tripwire in the background? Describe the shadow conflict or proxy crisis that the public knows nothing about, but which could trigger global retaliation if this op fails."

## STAGE 2: THE OPERATIVE, THE BURN & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Disavowed Operative + Stolen Nuclear Trigger + Urban Manhunt*, or *Lone Wolf Handler + Mole in the Agency + Cold War Retribution*). What stack are we deploying?"
6. "Who is our operative? What is their elite specialization (e.g., tactical CQB, social engineering/infiltrator, signals intelligence, or former tier-one operator), and what psychological scar makes them expendable to their superiors?"
7. "The Burn / Betrayal: How does our operative discover they have been disavowed, set up, or marked for terminal extraction? What was the kill order or frozen asset that signaled the ambush?"

## STAGE 3: THE MACGUFFIN & THE CLOCK
8. "What is the Core Asset or Weapon at the center of the manhunt? (e.g., an encrypted biometric key containing deep-cover agent lists, an EMP trigger, a biological pathogen, or a defecting scientist with state secrets?)."
9. "What is the Ticking Clock? Specify the hard deadline (e.g., 48 hours until the weapon goes live on a satellite orbit; 24 hours until the defector is handed over to a hostile black site)."
10. "Who is the Fixer / Black-Market Asset broker? What disreputable underworld intermediary does our operative have to deal with to secure weapons, forged papers, or off-grid transit?"

## STAGE 4: THE ADVERSARY & THE MOLE
11. "Who is the Field Hunter? Who is the adversary tracking our operative down? (e.g., a merciless state-sponsored assassin who knows our hero's tactics inside out, or an elite mercenary squad with limitless surveillance capabilities?)."
12. "The Agency Mole: Who inside headquarters or command is running interference, leaking the operative's locations, or sanitizing the files?"
13. "Who is the Reluctant Ally? (e.g., a local foreign intelligence agent with their own conflicting orders, a tech analyst trapped in the field, or an ex-lover whose trust was burned on a prior mission?)."

## STAGE 5: THE SIEGE & THE EXTRACTION
14. "The Midpoint Tactical Disaster: Where is the safehouse or exchange point where the plan catastrophically fails at 50%? How does the operative escape a three-sided crossfire through pure tactical improvisation?"
15. "The Dark Moment (75-80%): When all comms are cut, weapons are depleted, and the operative is wounded and cornered by their own government, what realization exposes the true architect of the conspiracy?"
16. "The Climax / Breach: How does the operative launch a counter-assault on the antagonist's fortified redoubt, turning the antagonist's own security protocol or electronic grid against them?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What pacing tempo and narrative point-of-view do you want? (e.g., relentless third-person close with precise millisecond action blocking; or split POV between the rogue operative in the mud and the CIA director in Langley watching satellite telemetry?)."
18. "How deep should the tactical and mechanical authenticity run? (e.g., authentic weapons mechanics, reload counts, ballistics, counter-surveillance routes, and cybersecurity realism)."
19. "What lingering moral ambiguity remains at the end? (Espionage thrillers rarely end in pure triumph—what is the price of keeping the world asleep for another day?)."

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an adrenaline-fueled, high-stakes tactical espionage architecture complete with tradecraft authenticity, lethal pacing, and high-level geopolitical conspiracies. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and an explosive commercial logline ready for an action thriller jacket.
