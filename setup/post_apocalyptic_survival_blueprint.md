# Post-Apocalyptic & Survival Fiction Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE ASHES, THE COLLAPSE & THE WASTELAND (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the ruins of civilization. Ask if they are ready to chronicle a survival odyssey where the power grid has failed, the old laws are dead, and every mouthful of clean water must be fought for.
2. "What was The Fall? Describe the cataclysm that ended the world (e.g., a continental EMP attack, a weaponized biological pandemic, nuclear exchange, ecological dust-bowl desolation, or an abrupt planetary freezing)."
3. "How long has passed since Day Zero? (e.g., Year 1: frantic scavengers picking through grocery store ruins and rotting suburbs; or Year 25: an overgrown wilderness where children believe pre-collapse airplanes were mythical metal birds)."
4. "The Sensory Viscosity of the Wasteland: Describe the environment (The taste of sulfur ash in the throat, the howling wind through broken concrete overpasses, rusted car skeletons on overgrown highways, the absolute, eerie silence of an empty world at night)."

## STAGE 2: THE SURVIVOR, THE VOW & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Parent & Child Odyssey + Desolate Frozen Highway + Cannibal Raider Threat*, or *Bunker Survivor + Poisoned Air Wasteland + Radio Signal Quest*). What stack are we carrying?"
6. "Who is our lead survivor? What was their mundane pre-collapse life, and what brutal survival skill or medical knowledge keeps them alive now?"
7. "The Sacred Burden / Vow: Who or what are they keeping alive? (e.g., a young child who has never seen electricity; an elderly mentor carrying seeds; or a fragile medical vial that could save an infected settlement)."

## STAGE 3: THE SCARCITY ENGINE & THE COUNTDOWN
8. "The Core Scarcity Dial: What resource is running out in Chapter 1? (e.g., their subterranean well has run dry; the last insulin pen is expired; or winter is arriving in three weeks and there is no fuel)."
9. "The Rumor of Sanctuary: What radio broadcast, map fragment, or nomad rumor sets them on the road? (e.g., a rumored colony on an offshore island, a government mountain bunker still running hydro-power, or a temperate valley south of the radiation zone)."
10. "The First Road Encounter: What moral test do they face on the highway in Act 1? (e.g., a starving family begging for food—do they share their dwindling rations or walk away to protect their own?)."

## STAGE 4: THE TRIBAL SCOURGE & THE NEW FEUDALISM
11. "Who are The Raiders / The Warlords? Describe the dominant predator faction stalking the wasteland (e.g., a motorized bandit gang draining fuel and taking slaves; a religious doomsday cult; or feral scavengers driven mad by hunger)."
12. "The Ruined Monument Set-Piece: What iconic pre-collapse landmark do they pass through or shelter in? (e.g., an abandoned airport terminal with rusted 747s, an overgrown shopping mall turned into a fortified barter town, or a subway station flooded with black water)."
13. "The Fragile Companion: Who joins them along the road, and what unexpected human warmth or humor do they bring to this cold landscape?"

## STAGE 5: THE SIEGE, THE LOSS & THE CROSSING
14. "The Midpoint Ambush / Loss: Where is their temporary sanctuary overrun at 50%? What vital piece of survival equipment (vehicle, rifle, water purifier) is lost or destroyed, reducing them to desperate foot travel?"
15. "The Dark Moment (75-80%): Wounded, hypothermic, or poisoned by toxic rain, when does our lead believe they can take another step? What profound emotional connection gives them the strength to crawl forward?"
16. "The Climax / Sanctuary Defense: How do they reach the perimeter of the sanctuary or outsmart the chasing warlord, using their deep knowledge of the wasteland's environmental dangers to turn the land itself into a weapon against their pursuers?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What prose style and pacing best fits the story? (Sparse, rhythmic, evocative prose reminiscent of Cormac McCarthy's *The Road*; or detailed, tactical, step-by-step survivalist realism?)."
18. "How will you handle violence and morality? (Ensuring violence is terrifying, messy, and traumatizing rather than sensationalized; keeping the preservation of human empathy at the center)."
19. "The Ending Horizon: Does the story conclude on arrival at a genuine haven, or an open road where the flame of human dignity has been successfully protected?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into a haunting, deeply moving post-apocalyptic survival architecture featuring visceral realism, tight resource stakes, and unforgettable emotional core. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a stirring commercial logline for a post-apocalyptic masterpiece.
