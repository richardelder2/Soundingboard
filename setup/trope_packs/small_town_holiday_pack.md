# Small-Town Holiday & Festival Modifier Pack

Use this pack to augment contemporary rom-com, cozy mystery, or cozy fantasy master blueprints with seasonal warmth, small-town rivalries, festival deadlines, and comfort aesthetics.

## 1. Commercial Trope Stacks & Sub-Genre Variants
- **Winter Holiday Romance:** Big City Executive Returns Home + Snowbound Cabin + Childhood Sweetheart / High School Rival + Saving the Family Tree Farm.
- **Autumn Harvest Festival:** Cider Mill Bake-Off + Grumpy Local Craftsman + Festival Organizing Committee + Pet Parade.
- **Summer Coastal Jubilee:** Boardwalk Renovation + Lobster Festival Mystery + Second Chance Childhood Fling.

## 2. In-World Sensory & Technical Lexicon
- **Tactile Details:** The scent of cinnamon, pine boughs, and woodsmoke; frost crunching under boots; steaming mugs of spiced cider with whipped cream; twinkling string lights reflected in frosty shop windows.
- **Terminology:** Town selectmen meeting, annual pageant, tree lighting countdown, gingerbread contest, community charity raffle, gazebo ribbon cutting.

## 3. Obligatory Reader Set-Pieces (Add to Ledger)
1. **The Awkward Hometown Reunion Meet-Cute (Ch. 1–3):** Bumping into the ex/rival at the local general store or diner while covered in snow or coffee.
2. **The Community Committee / Event Crisis (Ch. 8–10):** Forced to co-chair the holiday festival or save the failing community landmark together under tight town scrutiny.
3. **The Snowbound Cabin / Power Outage Isolation (Ch. 16–18):** Trapped together during a blizzard with one fireplace, hot cocoa, and emotional defenses crumbling.
4. **The Town-Square Festival Declaration (Ch. 28–30):** Public confession of love or dramatic triumph under the twinkling holiday lights as the entire town applauds.

## 4. Authenticity Pitfalls to Avoid
- *Keep the townsfolk eccentric, not cruel:* The community should feel like a warm, meddling found family rather than an antagonistic mob.
- *Earned career choices:* The protagonist's choice between the big city and small town should respect their genuine career ambitions.
