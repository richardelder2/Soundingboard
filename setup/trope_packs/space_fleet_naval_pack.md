# Space Fleet & Naval Warfare Modifier Pack

Use this pack to augment space opera or progression military sci-fi blueprints with authentic naval hierarchy, vector physics, electronic countermeasures, and tense bridge battle procedures.

## 1. Commercial Trope Stacks & Sub-Genre Variants
- **Capital Ship Dreadnought Duel:** Outgunned Heavy Cruiser + Gravity Well Trap + Tactical Nuclear Salvos.
- **Flight Wing Carrier Ops:** Fighter Squadron Comradeship + Planetary Flak Runs + High-G Evasive Maneuvers.
- **Stealth Recon / Submarine in Space:** Silent Running Past Imperial Sensor Grid + Passive Sonar/LIDAR + Boarding Action.

## 2. In-World Sensory & Technical Lexicon
- **Tactile Details:** The red strobe of battle stations, the clatter of magnetic boots on steel decking, the sharp ozone scent of laser point-defense capacitors, high-G crash foam pressing into ribs.
- **Terminology:** Reaction control thrusters, delta-V budget, kinetic penetrator, railgun battery, point-defense cannon (PDC), missile bus, heat dissipation radiators, gravimetric anomaly.

## 3. Obligatory Reader Set-Pieces (Add to Ledger)
1. **The 'Condition Red' Drill / Initial Ambush (Ch. 4–6):** Sirens wailing, bulkheads sealing, and the crew racing to stations as unknown contacts appear on long-range LIDAR.
2. **The High-G Vector Burn & Missile Intercept (Ch. 14–16):** A multi-million-kilometer tactical exchange where kinetic slugs and missiles cross orbits while PDC grids light up the void.
3. **The Hull Breach / Damage Control Heroics (Ch. 24–26):** Atmospheric venting, emergency fire suppression, and engineers risking decompression to keep the main reactor online.
4. **The Fleet Decisive Flank (Ch. 36–40):** An unorthodox orbital slingshot or tactical sacrifice that breaks the enemy fleet line and saves the homeworld.

## 4. Authenticity Pitfalls to Avoid
- *Space is not an ocean:* Ships don't bank like airplanes unless utilizing atmosphere; momentum persists, heat must be radiated, and ranges are measured in tens of thousands of kilometers.
- *Sensory realism:* Weapons in deep space make zero external sound—dread is communicated through hull vibrations and telemetry alarms.
