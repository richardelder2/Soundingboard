# Cyberpunk Street & Netrunning Modifier Pack

Use this pack to augment cyberpunk dystopia, heist caper, or noir detective blueprints with high-density hacking mechanics, street-level cybernetics, and corporate warfare jargon.

## 1. Commercial Trope Stacks & Sub-Genre Variants
- **Netrunner Cyberspace Infiltration:** Hot Deck Dive + Black ICE Defensive Grids + Rogue AI Extraction.
- **Street Merc / Chrome Overload:** Sub-Dermal Armor + Monomolecular Blades + Cyber-Psychosis Clock.
- **Bio-Hacker Guerrilla:** Underground Genetic Lab + Synthetic Virus Synthesis + Corporate Anti-Plague Patent Raid.

## 2. In-World Sensory & Technical Lexicon
- **Tactile Details:** Heat sinks humming against the base of the skull, blue sparks arcing from exposed neural jacks, chrome limbs reflecting saturated holographic advertisements, the chemical sting of synth-stimulants.
- **Terminology:** Cyberdeck, neuro-socket, Black ICE, sub-net proxy, sensory feed spoofing, daemon payload, wetware processor, ripperdoc clinic, biometric lockout.

## 3. Obligatory Reader Set-Pieces (Add to Ledger)
1. **The Ripperdoc Chair Installation (Ch. 3–5):** A gritty, painful cybernetic augmentation or repair scene in a subterranean clinic establishing the bodily cost of tech.
2. **The Deep Cyberspace Matrix Dive (Ch. 12–15):** The decker jacks into the corporate grid, visualizing data architectures as towering geometric monoliths while fighting off defensive ICE.
3. **The Street Shootout with Chrome Overclock (Ch. 20–24):** Reflex boosters activated in slow-motion kinetic combat where cyberware begins to overheat dangerously.
4. **The Broadcast / Corporate System Wipe (Ch. 30–34):** Overriding citywide transmission arrays to leak classified corporate files directly onto citizens' retinal feeds.

## 4. Authenticity Pitfalls to Avoid
- *Hacking is not magic:* Establish clear protocols, physical server locations, encryption hurdles, and time limits so hacking retains high suspense.
- *Preserve the human stakes:* Cyberpunk works when the struggle is over the soul, dignity, and survival of marginalized people against an unfeeling machine.
