# Courtroom & Legal Procedure Modifier Pack

Use this pack to augment legal thriller, noir detective, or domestic thriller master blueprints with procedural accuracy, statutory drama, and intense evidentiary battles.

## 1. Commercial Trope Stacks & Sub-Genre Variants
- **Capital Murder Defense:** Indigent Defense Attorney + Framing Conspiracy + Hostile Presiding Judge.
- **Corporate Mass Tort / Whistleblower:** Solo Practitioner vs Big Pharma Monolith + Tampered Discovery Documents + Vanishing Witnesses.
- **Wrongful Conviction Appeal:** Ticking Clock on Death Row + Forensic Ballistics Re-investigation + Corrupt Former DA.

## 2. In-World Sensory & Technical Lexicon
- **Tactile Details:** The heavy thud of the judge's wooden gavel, dry smell of Manila folders and microfiche, squeak of counsel shoes on linoleum approaches, the tense rustle of the jury entering the box.
- **Terminology:** Motion in limine, Brady violation, chain of custody, voir dire, sidebar conference, hearsay exception, impeachment of witness, directed verdict, Allen charge.

## 3. Obligatory Reader Set-Pieces (Add to Ledger)
1. **The Suppression Hearing / Motion in Limine (Ch. 6–8):** A vicious preliminary clash where key physical evidence is nearly thrown out by the defense.
2. **The Voir Dire / Jury Selection Gamble (Ch. 10–12):** Picking the 12 jurors while identifying a plant or biased juror slipped in by opposing counsel.
3. **The Devastating Cross-Examination (Ch. 22–26):** Trapping a hostile expert witness or corrupt detective in a documented contradiction on the witness stand.
4. **The Jury Deliberation Ticking Clock (Ch. 32–35):** The agonized wait in the hallway, notes sent out from the deliberation room, and the dramatic reading of the verdict.

## 4. Authenticity Pitfalls to Avoid
- *No Perry Mason confessions out of nowhere:* Real courtroom breakthroughs come from documented discrepancies, bank records, and statutory traps, not sudden emotional breakdowns on the stand.
- *Strict judicial limits:* Judges do not tolerate prolonged theatrical speeches during witness questioning—objections are rapid, sharp, and ruled on instantly.
