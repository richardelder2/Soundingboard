# SERIES BIBLE & TEMPLATE: Upmarket Fiction & Historical Family Saga
*Two templates for character-driven emotional fiction: (A) The **Upmarket Domestic Drama** (~85k words, ~32 chapters of moral dilemma, community secrets, and psychological reckoning), and (B) The **Multi-Generational Historical Saga** (~95k words, ~38 chapters of family dynasty, historical inflection points, and enduring legacy).*

# TEMPLATE A: UPMARKET DOMESTIC DRAMA

- **The Core Moral Dilemma:** The choice with no clean answer [___] · why both sides have profound justification [___]
- **The Catalyst Secret:** The event that shatters the curated peace in Chapter 1 [___] · the cover-up lie [___]

## A2. BEAT SHEET — Chapter Skeleton (~85k words, ~32 chapters of ~2,600 words)
- **Act 1 (ch. 1–8): The Curated Surface & The Catalyst.** Life looks enviable; children thriving; dinner parties; sudden event strikes; protagonist tells the initial lie to shield her family.
- **Act 2A (ch. 9–16): The Ripple Effect & Whispers.** The lie requires three more small lies; friends begin taking sides; the protagonist's spouse notices discrepancies; school/workplace friction escalates.
- **Act 2B (ch. 17–24): The Public Rupture & Confrontation.** Midpoint dinner party or community assembly where the accusation becomes public; allies withdraw; husband feels betrayed; marriage fractures.
- **Act 3 (ch. 25–32): The Reckoning & Bittersweet Healing.** All evasions fail; protagonist confronts the victim or antagonist directly; an honest confession clears the air at great social cost; a poignant, realistic path forward begins.

# TEMPLATE B: MULTI-GENERATIONAL HISTORICAL SAGA

## B1. SERIES BIBLE (fill-in)
- **The Family Dynasty:** Family name [___] · the trade or estate at their heart [vineyard / shipping / textile mill] · founding sin or secret debt [___]
- **The Historical Crucible:** Macro-historical era [1914–1945 / Gilded Age / Great Depression] · how historical events buffet the family [___]
- **The Rival Clan:** Competing family or industrial baron [___]

## B2. BEAT SHEET — Chapter Skeleton (~95k words, ~38 chapters of ~2,500 words)
- **Act 1 (ch. 1–9): The Patriarch's Rise & The Legacy.** Establishing the family business; sibling rivalries seeded early; the patriarch's strict code; ominous rumble of approaching historical crisis.
- **Act 2A (ch. 10–19): The Crucible of War / Crash.** The historical catastrophe hits; factory requisitioned or estate bankrupted; sons sent to the front; daughters taking unprecedented leadership roles.
- **Act 2B (ch. 20–29): Sibling Fractures & The Buried Truth.** Betrayal between siblings over inheritance and war contracts; the founding family secret surfaces; the family estate is threatened with foreclosure.
- **Act 3 (ch. 30–38): The Rebuilding & Generational Reconciliation.** Surviving generation unites to save the ancestral land; profound personal sacrifice made; the torch passed to the grandchildren; the enduring family name survives against history.
