## 2. BOOK BEAT SHEET — Chapter Skeleton (~75k words, ~30 chapters of ~2,500 words)

**ACT 1: The Arrival & The Deniable Manifestations (ch. 1–7, 0–25%)**
- Ch. 1: Crossing the Threshold — arrival at the isolated estate; atmospheric gloom; eerie architecture.
- Ch. 2–3: The First Night — settling in; the acoustic ecology of the house (settling wood, pipe groans, cold draft).
- Ch. 4–5: Wrongness #1 (Obligatory #1) — finding objects displaced; catching a shadow in a mirror; explaining it away.
- Ch. 6–7: The Local Warning — villager or groundskeeper reveals a snippet of the house's dark history; lead dismisses it as superstition.

**ACT 2A: The Isolation & The Psychological Rot (ch. 8–15, 25–50%)**
- Ch. 8–9: The Severed Link — storm washes out the access bridge or phone service cuts out; total physical isolation.
- Ch. 10–12: Escalating Manifestations — distinct sounds of footsteps pacing overhead; nightmares blending with waking reality; waking with mud on nightclothes.
- Ch. 13–14: Paranoia & Hallucinations — lead begins doubting their own senses; finding antique diary pages in floorboards.
- Ch. 15: **MIDPOINT: The Hidden Chamber (Obligatory #2):** Discovering the sealed cellar or secret attic room; undeniable physical evidence of occult atrocities; the haunting is undeniably real.

**ACT 2B: The Siege & Cognitive Fracture (ch. 16–22, 50–75%)**
- Ch. 16–17: The House Strikes — doors locking from the outside; reflections refusing to move; temperatures dropping to freezing.
- Ch. 18–19: Bodily Assault / Possession — entity physically attacks or influences the lead's thoughts; exhaustion and terror.
- Ch. 20–21: Desperate Attempt at Flight — trying to flee on foot through the storm; the road loops impossibly back to the manor gates.
- Ch. 22: **The 75% Darkest Hour (Obligatory #3):** Cornered in the central hall; the lead realizes the house's entity wants them to replicate the original murder-suicide; total surrender seems near.

**ACT 3: The Pyre & The Ambiguous Escape (ch. 23–30, 75–100%)**
- Ch. 23–25: The Fight for Sanity — clinging to a physical anchor or memory of real love; preparing the house's destruction.
- Ch. 26–27: Setting the Blaze — dousing the malevolent timbers in kerosene; the house physically fights to extinguish the fire.
- Ch. 28–29: **The Climax & Breakout (Obligatory #4):** Crawling through shattered stained glass as the roof collapses into flame; watching the estate burn against the dawn sky.
- Ch. 30: **The Haunting Aftermath:** Sitting in an ambulance or motel room; safe at last—until the final line reveals a subtle, terrifying sensory cue that something of the house came away inside them.
