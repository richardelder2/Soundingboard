## 2. BOOK BEAT SHEET — Chapter Skeleton (~80k words, ~32 chapters of ~2,500 words)

**ACT 1: The Season & The Bargain (ch. 1–8, 0–25%)**
- Ch. 1: Heroine at Almack's or opening assembly; social pressure to marry; her family's financial peril made clear.
- Ch. 2: The Collision — explosive, witty verbal clash with the Hero in a quiet terrace or library.
- Ch. 3: The Scandalous Encounter — witnessed together in a compromising shadow; society begins to murmur.
- Ch. 4–5: The Proposition — Hero visits her home; offers a fake courtship to solve both their problems.
- Ch. 6–7: Establishing the Rules — no real feelings; boundaries set; public appearances mapped out.
- Ch. 8: **The First Public Waltz (Obligatory #1):** Dancing together at a major ball; the Ton is stunned; physical chemistry shatters their rational detachment.

**ACT 2A: The Charade & The Vulnerability (ch. 9–16, 25–50%)**
- Ch. 9–11: Afternoon Promenade in Hyde Park — bantering in his high-perch phaeton; learning his genuine passion.
- Ch. 12–13: The Rival Suitor — a respectable, safe gentleman begins courting her, sparking the Hero's irrational jealousy.
- Ch. 14–15: Country House Party Invitation — forced into overnight country house proximity in Yorkshire.
- Ch. 16: **MIDPOINT: The Snapped Tension (Obligatory #2):** Caught in a sudden rainstorm; taking shelter in a rustic pavilion; passionate first kiss. Immediate emotional panic as real vulnerability surfaces.

**ACT 2B: Deepening Passion & The Impending Ruin (ch. 17–24, 50–75%)**
- Ch. 17–19: Private intimacy behind closed doors; exploring the country estate; sharing childhood wounds.
- Ch. 20–21: Hero defends her against cruel high-society snobs at the house banquet (Obligatory #3).
- Ch. 22–23: Passion escalates to consummation / high heat according to chosen heat ladder.
- Ch. 24: **The Third-Act Rupture / The Gossip Detonation (Obligatory #4):** The fake courtship contract is discovered or leaked to the gossip sheet; Hero misinterprets her motives as fortune hunting; cruel words spoken; heroine retreats to London in disgrace.

**ACT 3: The Grand Declaration & The HEA (ch. 25–32, 75–100%)**
- Ch. 25–26: The Agony of Separation — heroine prepares to take a governess post in the north; Hero realizes his life is empty without her.
- Ch. 27–28: Hero confronts the source of the leak, uncovering his former mistress or rival's treachery.
- Ch. 29–30: **The Grand Ballroom Declaration (Obligatory #5):** Hero rides through the night, breaches the final grand ball of the Season, and confesses his unconditional love before the entire assembly, kneeling before her.
- Ch. 31: Acceptance & Joy — joyous reconciliation and passionate reunion.
- Ch. 32: **Epilogue:** Domestic happiness at his ancestral estate; sweet or spicy domestic bliss; setup for Book 2's sibling.
