## 2. BOOK BEAT SHEET — Chapter Skeleton (~140k words, ~52 chapters of ~2,700 words)

**ACT 1: The Gathering Storm (ch. 1–13, 0–25%)**
- Ch. 1–2 (POV 1): Frontline skirmish reveals the enemy is wielding ancient, forbidden sorcery.
- Ch. 3–4 (POV 2): Imperial capital intrigue; royal assassination sparks nationwide martial law.
- Ch. 5–6 (POV 3): Accidental awakening of dangerous magic in a remote farming village; fleeing the Inquisition.
- Ch. 7–8 (POV 4): Rebel camp internal strife; planning a raid on an imperial supply train.
- Ch. 9–10 (POV 1 & 2): Military orders arrive that directly contradict diplomatic peace negotiations.
- Ch. 11–13: **Act 1 Climax / Declaration of War (Obligatory #1):** Imperial borders breached simultaneously; all four leads are thrust into active conflict.

**ACT 2A: The Fracture & The March (ch. 14–26, 25–50%)**
- Ch. 14–16 (POV 1): Leading a tactical retreat through hostile mountain passes; supply shortages.
- Ch. 17–19 (POV 2): Discovering that the royal succession has been compromised by an elder cult.
- Ch. 20–22 (POV 3): Seeking an ancient ruined archive to learn how to control the volatile magical power.
- Ch. 23–25 (POV 4): Rebel raid succeeds, but exposes a weapon of mass destruction being assembled by their own side.
- Ch. 26: **MIDPOINT CONTINENTAL CATACLYSM (Obligatory #2):** A major fortress falls; ancient magic detonates, ripping the regional sky open and shifting all troop movements toward the Central Citadel.

**ACT 2B: Desperate Counter-Offensives (ch. 27–39, 50–75%)**
- Ch. 27–29 (POV 1): Under siege inside the mountain pass; fighting off overwhelming odds with brilliant engineering.
- Ch. 30–32 (POV 2): Escaping the capital with the child-heir after the council is purged; heading toward the Frontline.
- Ch. 33–35 (POV 3): Mastering the magic's core rule at immense personal cost; setting out to intercept the weapon.
- Ch. 36–38 (POV 4): Rebel defection; trying to warn the imperial garrison before the slaughter begins.
- Ch. 39: **The 75% Disaster / Total Collapse (Obligatory #3):** The villain's army breaches the outer walls; the alliance shatters; every lead suffers a catastrophic defeat.

**ACT 3: The Sanderlanche Convergence (ch. 40–52, 75–100%)**
- Ch. 40–42: The 4 POVs reach the same valley/citadel from four different compass directions.
- Ch. 43–45: POV 1 holds the gate against the vanguard while POV 2 secures the defensive wards inside the throne room.
- Ch. 46–48: POV 4 sabotages the enemy's siege engine from behind the lines, creating the opening POV 3 needs to unleash the ancient magic.
- Ch. 49–50: **The Polyphonic Climax (Obligatory #4):** The magic system's hard rule is tested; the leads achieve victory through synchronized sacrifice and interlocking tactical turns.
- Ch. 51: The Aftermath & The Dead: Counting the cost on the battlefield; leaders face the shattered political landscape.
- Ch. 52: Resolution & The Next Horizon: A new monarch crowned or alliance forged; a distant omen teases the greater cosmic threat for Book 2.
