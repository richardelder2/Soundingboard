# SERIES BIBLE & TEMPLATE: Crime Noir & Heist Caper
*Two distinct templates for criminal fiction: (A) The **Intricate 5-Phase Heist** (~80k words, ~32 chapters of specialist planning, vault penetration, and double-bluff payoffs), and (B) The **Hardboiled Noir Downward Spiral** (~75k words, ~30 chapters of institutional rot, cigarette-smoke cynicism, and bittersweet personal integrity).*

---

# TEMPLATE A: 5-PHASE HEIST & SCORE

## A1. BOOK BIBLE (fill-in)
- **The Score:** Target asset [rare jewel / bearer bonds / prototype tech / museum relic] · value [___] · location [casino vault / private island / gallery]
- **The Mark / Target:** Corrupt owner [___] · why they deserve to be stripped of their fortune [___] · security chief [___]
- **The Crew (Roles & Quirks):**
  - The Mastermind / Face: [___]
  - The Safecracker / Acrobat: [___]
  - The Tech / Systems Decker: [___]
  - The Muscle / Distraction: [___]
  - The Inside Asset: [___]
- **The Fortification Rules:** 3 security layers that make standard entry suicide [laser grid / seismic sensors / biometric timers].
- **The Double-Bluff:** What disaster in Act 3 is revealed to be the crew's real plan all along? [___]

## A2. BEAT SHEET — Chapter Skeleton (~80k words, ~32 chapters of ~2,500 words)

**ACT 1: The Gathering & The Blueprint (ch. 1–8, 0–25%)**
- Ch. 1: The Mastermind's Grievance — why this mark, why now. One small solo demonstration score.
- Ch. 2–3: Recruiting the Specialist Crew — tracking down the safecracker, hacker, and muscle across underworld haunts.
- Ch. 4–5: Casing the Joint — visiting the venue under false pretenses; mapping security cameras and guard patrol intervals.
- Ch. 6–7: Acquiring the Blueprints & Countermeasures — black-market purchase or mini-heist to get vault schematics.
- Ch. 8: **The Dry Run (Obligatory #1):** A timed simulation reveals the fatal flaw in the crew's timing. Friction between crew members.

**ACT 2A: The Infiltration & The First Complication (ch. 9–16, 25–50%)**
- Ch. 9–10: Gala / Public Event Setup — crew assumes their aliases at the black-tie gala.
- Ch. 11–12: Phase 1: Infiltration — slipping past outer perimeter security; hacking the digital camera loop.
- Ch. 13–14: Phase 2: System Jamming — triggering the staged distraction in the ballroom (spilled champagne, fire alarm, power flicker).
- Ch. 15–16: **MIDPOINT: The Vault Breach (Obligatory #2):** Safecracker reaches the inner door and cracks the impossible lock. Asset secured! But the security system unexpectedly resets with new protocols.

**ACT 2B: The Complication Cascade & The Trap (ch. 17–24, 50–75%)**
- Ch. 17–18: Phase 3: The Trap Springs — alarms trip; security chief seals all elevators and emergency stairwells.
- Ch. 19–20: The Improvised Egress — muscle detonates secondary charges; acrobat takes the elevator shaft cable.
- Ch. 21–22: The Apparent Betrayal — the inside asset appears to turn coat and leads the security team right to the extraction point.
- Ch. 23–24: **The 75% Dark Moment (Obligatory #3):** The mastermind is cornered at gunpoint; the briefcase containing the asset is seized by the mark. The heist is declared an utter failure.

**ACT 3: The Double-Bluff & The Clean Escape (ch. 25–32, 75–100%)**
- Ch. 25–26: The Police Swarm & The Mark's Arrogance — the mark mocks the captured mastermind while moving the recovered asset to his private limousine.
- Ch. 27–28: **THE REVEAL / DOUBLE-BLUFF (Obligatory #4):** Flashback sequence showing the crew switched the real vault contents during Phase 2; the captured asset was a decoy loaded with GPS trackers and confession mics!
- Ch. 29–30: The Mark's Downfall — federal authorities arrest the mark based on the contents of his own limousine; mastermind slips away in an ambulance.
- Ch. 31–32: The Split & Celebration — the crew reassembles on a tropical beach or safehouse; shares distributed; toast to the next target.

# TEMPLATE B: HARDBOILDED NOIR DOWNWARD SPIRAL

## B1. BOOK BIBLE (fill-in)
- **The City:** Name [___] · specific rot [dock corruption / political mafia / vice syndicates] · sensory anchor [rain / neon / smog]
- **The PI:** Name [___] · veteran code [___] · the wound that made him cynical [___]
- **The Client & The Lie:** Who walks into the office [___] · what simple tracking job they claim to want [___] · the radioactive reality [___]
- **The Fatale:** Name [___] · attraction hook [___] · the betrayal they are forced to commit [___]

## B2. BEAT SHEET — Chapter Skeleton (~75k words, ~30 chapters of ~2,500 words)
- **Act 1 (ch. 1–7): The Retainer & The First Corpse.** Frosty office door; glamorous client with bruises; first surveillance run leads straight to a murder scene where the PI is framed.
- **Act 2A (ch. 8–15): The Shakedown & The Vice Web.** Corrupt homicide detectives deliver an alley beating; PI uncovers blackmail photographs linking city hall to mob syndicate docks.
- **Act 2B (ch. 16–22): The Broken Code & The Betrayal.** Femme fatale confesses her complicity in bed; the client is murdered; the PI realizes the entire city apparatus is complicit.
- **Act 3 (ch. 23–30): The Bitter Showdown & Pyrrhic Truth.** Confronting the untouchable syndicate boss in his penthouse; the boss cannot be prosecuted, so the PI executes personal justice or ensures the truth reaches the press; walking away into the rain with bruised ribs and a clean soul.
