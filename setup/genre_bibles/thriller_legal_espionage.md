## A2. BEAT SHEET — Chapter Skeleton (~85k words, ~36 chapters of ~2,400 words)

**ACT 1 (ch. 1–9, 0–25%): The Retainer & Pre-Trial Motions**
- Ch. 1: The Arrest / The Retainer — defense attorney meets the accused in lockup. First assessment of credibility.
- Ch. 2–3: Bail Hearing & Arraignment — high-stakes bail hearing before a hostile judge. Media circus on the steps.
- Ch. 4–5: Discovery Dump — reviewing police files and autopsy reports; finding the initial discrepancy in chain of custody.
- Ch. 6–7: The Street Investigation — lead investigator interviews an uncooperative street witness; first threat delivered to the defense office.
- Ch. 8–9: **Motion in Limine Battle (Obligatory #1)** — courtroom clash over evidence suppression. Partial victory, but defense is boxed in.

**ACT 2A (ch. 10–18, 25–50%): The State's Case & Voir Dire**
- Ch. 10–11: Voir Dire — jury selection dynamics; seating the controversial juror who will hold the key.
- Ch. 12–13: Opening Statements — prosecution paints a terrifying picture; defense stakes its credibility on reasonable doubt.
- Ch. 14–15: The Medical Examiner / Forensic Witness — defense cross-examines the forensic expert, exposing a flawed scientific methodology.
- Ch. 16–17: The Lead Detective's Testimony — defense establishes tunnel vision and missed investigative leads.
- Ch. 18: **MIDPOINT REVERSAL (Obligatory #2):** The Prosecution's Surprise Witness drops an unexpected bomb that shatters the defense theory. Client confesses their secret in lockup.

**ACT 2B (ch. 19–27, 50–75%): The Defense's Offensive**
- Ch. 19–20: Emergency Motions & Scramble — defense scrambles off-grid to verify the client's confession and find corroboration.
- Ch. 21–22: The Break-in / Office Tampering — dark forces attempt to steal or intimidate the defense's remaining proof.
- Ch. 23–24: Putting on the Defense Case — calling the overlooked witness; establishing the alternate suspect's motive.
- Ch. 25–26: The Accused Takes the Stand — high-stakes direct examination and brutal prosecution cross-examination; client holds their nerve.
- Ch. 27: **The 75% Disaster (Obligatory #3):** Judge excludes a crucial defense witness on a procedural technicality. All seems lost.

**ACT 3 (ch. 28–36, 75–100%): The Rebuttal, Closings & The Verdict**
- Ch. 28–29: Overnight Discovery Breakthrough — investigator finds the missing financial record or security footage hidden in plain sight.
- Ch. 30–31: Rebuttal Cross-Examination — recall of the key witness; attorney executes a flawless, trap-door cross-examination proving the conspiracy.
- Ch. 32–33: Closing Arguments — prosecution's desperate appeal to emotion; defense delivers an unforgettable masterclass on the presumption of innocence.
- Ch. 34: Jury Deliberations — tense waiting, jury questions sent to the bench, the knock on the deliberation room door.
- Ch. 35: **The Verdict Delivered on Page (Obligatory #4):** "Not Guilty" read aloud; courtroom erupts; real perpetrator arrested in the gallery.
- Ch. 36: Resolution & Denouement — quiet drink in the empty office; final reconciliation with personal life.
