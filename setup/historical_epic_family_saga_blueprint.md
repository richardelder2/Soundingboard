# Historical Epic & Generational Family Saga Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE ERA, THE EMPIRE & HISTORICAL CURRENTS (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the grand chronicle of history. Ask if they are ready to construct a sweeping historical saga where intimate human passions and family secrets are forged against the anvil of great historical events.
2. "What is our Historical Era & Primary Geography? (e.g., the building of a great cathedral in 12th-century England; the turbulent rise of a merchant family in 1920s Shanghai; or a farming dynasty surviving the American Dust Bowl and World War II)."
3. "The Sensory Viscosity of Period Authenticity: Describe the textures, sounds, and smells of this historical world (The smell of woodsmoke, horses, coal-dust, or salt air; the texture of homespun linen vs imported damask silk; the rhythm of church bells and market cries)."
4. "The Macro-Historical Crucible: What major real-world historical event (war, revolution, plague, economic depression, religious schism) acts as the unstoppable tide reshaping the characters' lives?"

## STAGE 2: THE FAMILY DYNASTY & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Immigrant Ambition + Industrial Gilded Age + Sibling Rivalry*, or *Forbidden Love Across Class Lines + Wartime Occupation + Lost Heirlooms*). What saga are we writing?"
6. "The Family Name & Founding Dream: What is the family name, and what is the patriarch/matriarch's founding ambition or trade? (e.g., master stone masons, vineyard owners, shipping magnates, silk weavers)."
7. "The Original Sin or Buried Debt: What morally questionable compromise, stolen land, or secret shame was the family's fortune built upon at the very beginning?"

## STAGE 3: THE GENERATIONS & SIBLING RIVALRIES
8. "Introduce the Central Generation / Leads:
   - Lead 1 (The Ambitious Heir / Dreamer):
   - Lead 2 (The Rebel / Outcast / Artist):
   - Lead 3 (The Dutiful Anchor / Protector):"
9. "The Sibling Friction: What long-simmering resentment or rivalry divides the children? (Favoritism from the parents, competing for the same inheritance, or loving the same person)."
10. "The Generational Shift: How do the values of the new generation clash violently with the traditional customs and religious expectations of the elders?"

## STAGE 4: THE OPPOSING DYNASTY & SOCIAL HIERARCHY
11. "The Rival Clan / Opposing Force: Who is the competing dynasty, land baron, or political oppressor trying to break our family's power?"
12. "The Forbidden Romance: Is there a star-crossed romance across historical divides (religious, class, racial, or national) that threatens the family's standing?"
13. "The Physical Anchor / The Homeland Estate: What physical property, house, factory, or valley represents the family's soul, which they will bleed to defend?"

## STAGE 5: THE WAR, THE RUIN & RECONSTRUCTION
14. "The Midpoint Historical Shock (50%): When the historical catastrophe (war outbreak, market crash, invasion) strikes at the halfway mark, how is the family's fortune shattered overnight?"
15. "The Dark Moment (75-80%): Following imprisonment, exile, or the death of a beloved figure, what secret comes to light that threatens to permanently tear the surviving family members apart?"
16. "The Climax / Generational Reconciliation: How do the survivors unite or make a profound sacrifice to save the family name, protect their community, and pass the torch to the next generation?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What temporal span and narrative structure will you employ? (A single dramatic year framed by a war; or an expansive 30-year multi-generational timeline structured into distinct historical acts in the tradition of Ken Follett, Hilary Mantel, and Min Jin Lee?)."
18. "How does the story balance rigorous historical fidelity with engaging character agency? (Ensuring historical research enriches the characters' dilemmas without turning into an encyclopedia)."
19. "The Poignant Ending: What enduring family legacy or quiet heirloom survives the passage of time in the final pages?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an emotionally profound, panoramic historical epic architecture rich with period texture, complex family dynamics, and immense dramatic weight. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a sweeping commercial logline worthy of a historical fiction bestseller.
