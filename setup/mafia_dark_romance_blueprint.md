# Mafia & Dark Contemporary Romance Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE SYNDICATE, THE COMPOUND & UNDERWORLD CODE (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the underworld. Ask if they are ready to engineer an intense, morally complex dark romance where vows are sealed in blood, danger lurks behind blacked-out SUVs, and passion is a high-stakes power struggle.
2. "What is our Underworld Syndicate / Family? Describe the organization, its territory, and its business operations (e.g., the Italian Cosa Nostra controlling New York ports; the Russian Bratva running Las Vegas nightlife; or rival cartels in Chicago)."
3. "The Sensory Viscosity of the Mafia World: (The smell of expensive cigars, gunpowder residue, and Italian espresso; the hum of security gates closing on an armored estate; tailored Armani suits concealing shoulder holsters; marble penthouses with private security cameras)."
4. "The Omertà & The Blood Law: What is the cardinal law of the family that no member may break on pain of death? What happens when our heroine's presence directly challenges that law?"

## STAGE 2: THE LEADS, THE POWER BALANCE & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Arranged Marriage + Mafia Don + Touch Her and Die*, or *Captive Princess + Rival Enforcer + Forbidden Protection Code*). What stack are we igniting?"
6. "Who is our Hero (The Don / Enforcer)? What is his reputation across the five families? (e.g., a cold, ruthless Capo known as 'The Reaper' who feels no remorse—until he is assigned to protect or claim our heroine)."
7. "Who is our Heroine? What is her strength and agency? (Crucial: In dark romance, she cannot be merely a passive victim—she must possess fierce emotional endurance, tactical intelligence, or a secret that gives her leverage over him)."

## STAGE 3: THE ARRANGED MARRIAGE / THE CLAIM
8. "The Blood Pact / Transaction: What crisis forces their union in Act 1? (e.g., an arranged marriage to end a gang war between rival families; payment for an unpaid father's debt; or a strategic alliance ordered by the Commission)."
9. "The Wedding / Claiming Scene: Describe the high-tension wedding or public introduction. What silent vows or warnings are exchanged when the gold ring is placed on her finger?"
10. "The Forced Proximity / The Fortress: Describe the master bedroom or wing where she is sequestered. How does she test the perimeter, defy his guards, and refuse to bow to his command?"

## STAGE 4: THE THREAT FROM OUTSIDE & THE RIVAL FAMILY
11. "Who is the External Threat? Which rival boss, corrupt federal agent, or traitorous cousin is seeking to assassinate the hero or kidnap the heroine to break him?"
12. "The 'Touch Her and Die' Signature Moment: What scene early in the book shows the hero visiting unholy, disproportionate violence upon someone who dares disrespect, threaten, or lay a finger on her?"
13. "The Loyal Soldiers / The Lieutenants: Who are the 2 loyal enforcers or brothers who guard the estate, and how do they slowly come to respect the new queen?"

## STAGE 5: THE BETRAYAL, THE WAR & THE GROVEL / DEVOTION
14. "The Midpoint Shift / Tension Break: Where does the animosity between them erupt into raw physical passion at 50%? How does intimacy completely shift the power dynamic in the house?"
15. "The Dark Moment (75-80%): What betrayal surfaces? (e.g., she discovers he had a hand in her family's downfall, or she is framed for leaking syndicate secrets to the FBI, causing him to doubt her for one fatal moment)."
16. "The Blood-Soaked Climax: When she is taken or cornered by the true enemy, how does the hero tear through an entire army to reclaim her? How does her own decisive action turn the tide and prove she is his true equal?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What heat level and POV structure will you use? (Dual first-person present POV is the commercial gold standard; high spice, primal tension, and explicit erotic pacing balanced with emotional vulnerability)."
18. "Content Warnings & Emotional Safety: What dark themes and boundary edges will you navigate? (Establishing clear boundaries between consensual darkness, primal kink, and genuine emotional devotion)."
19. "The HEA & The Coronation: How does the book end with them ruling the family together as undisputed king and queen of the underworld?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an addictive, heart-racing dark mafia romance blueprint loaded with primal chemistry, unbreakable protective instincts, and gripping mob war tension. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and an intoxicating commercial blurb hook.
