# Legal & Courtroom Thriller Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE INSTITUTIONAL ARENA & SENSORY GRAVITY (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the legal litigation chambers. Ask if they are ready to construct a trial where the truth is dangerous, procedure is a weapon, and an innocent life or massive conspiracy hangs in the balance.
2. "What is the jurisdiction, venue, and physical atmosphere of this courtroom? (e.g., a cavernous, wood-paneled federal courthouse in Manhattan smelling of old legal leather and humidity; a strained county courthouse in rural Georgia where everyone knows the judge's family; or a high-security military tribunal?)."
3. "What are the institutional stakes and systemic rot in the background? Describe the political pressure surrounding the court—e.g., a looming district attorney election, corporate monopolies funding judicial campaigns, or media swarms camped on the courthouse steps."
4. "What is the physical toll of trial preparation on the team? (The cold takeout containers, the late-night fluorescent glare over bankers boxes of discovery documents, the paper cuts, the coffee-fueled adrenaline and sleep deprivation)."

## STAGE 2: THE CASE, THE ACCUSED & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Underdog Defense vs. Corrupt State + Smoking Gun Discovery + Personal Vendetta*, or *Burnout Solo Practitioner + High-Profile Capital Murder + Whistleblower Betrayal*). What stack are we arguing?"
6. "Who is the client/accused, and what is the public narrative damning them? What is the undeniable piece of circumstantial or physical evidence that makes them look 100% guilty to the public?"
7. "What is the client's fatal secret? What is the one thing they refuse to tell their own defense attorney until it detonates mid-trial?"

## STAGE 3: THE OPPOSITION & THE TRIAL BENCH
8. "Who is the Lead Prosecutor / Opposing Counsel? What is their litigation style? (e.g., the calm, theatrical orator who charms juries; the relentless procedural assassin who buries the defense in motions in limine; or a ruthless former mentor?)."
9. "Introduce His/Her Honor: Who is the presiding judge? What is their idiosyncratic temperament, judicial prejudice, or bench rule that keeps both attorneys walking a tightrope?"
10. "What is the Smoking-Gun Discovery or Evidence Fight? What crucial document, surveillance tape, or forensic report is fighting for admissibility during pre-trial hearings?"

## STAGE 4: THE DEFENSE TEAM & THE INVESTIGATIVE RUNWAY
11. "Who is our lead attorney? What is their signature tactical strength in court, and what past professional failure or ethical scar makes this case personal?"
12. "Who is the lead Investigator / Paralegal on the ground? What dangerous off-the-record tradecraft or street-level digging do they perform while counsel is stuck in court?"
13. "What ethical crossroads does the attorney face before opening statements? What temptation to cut corners, bend discovery rules, or betray a boundary tests their moral compass?"

## STAGE 5: THE CROSS-EXAMINATION & THE JURY CLOCK
14. "The Signature Witness Battle: Who is the star prosecution witness, and what seems airtight about their testimony? How does our attorney dismantle them on cross-examination using Swain MRU pacing and trapped logic?"
15. "The Crisis at the Close of Prosecution's Case: What sudden bench ruling, suppressed witness, or manufactured crisis makes the defense's acquittal seem mathematically impossible at the 75% mark?"
16. "The Rebuttal Breakthrough / Closing Argument: What overlooked detail in the physical record or last-minute discovery provides the key to unlocking the true conspiracy during the final hours of jury deliberation?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What narrative structure best fits the case? (e.g., linear countdown through jury selection, opening statements, evidence, and verdict; or a dual timeline alternating between the crime that occurred and the courtroom battle today?)."
18. "How does the story handle legal jargon and procedure? (Authentic procedural tension should rise organically from statutory rules, objections, and bench conferences without pausing for dry textbook exposition)."
19. "What personal B-plot is crumbling outside the courtroom? (e.g., an estranged marriage, health issues, or an orphaned nephew that provides grounding emotional counterpoint to the legal war)."

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into a taut, compelling legal thriller architecture featuring an unyielding institutional battle, fair-play evidence tracking, and explosive courtroom confrontations. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a punchy commercial logline suitable for a legal thriller bestseller jacket.
