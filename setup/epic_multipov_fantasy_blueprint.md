# Epic Multi-POV High Fantasy Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE CONTINENT, THE COSMOLOGY & THE AGES (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the Grand Archive. Ask if they are ready to chronicle an epic continental saga where empires clash, ancient covenants fracture, and multiple mortal lives collide against destiny.
2. "What is the Continent or Realm? Describe the major geographic biomes, physical landmarks, and ancient ruins that shape trade routes and military choke points (e.g., a spine of shatter-glass mountains, an arid basin ruled by nomadic canal-builders, or an archipelago of floating stone citadels)."
3. "What is the Cosmology and Ancient History? What primordial event, cataclysm, or war of gods shattered the previous golden age, leaving behind radioactive magical fallout, dead deities, or forgotten oaths?"
4. "The Magic System & Sanderson's Laws:
   - What can this magic DO (the kinetic or spiritual manifestation)?
   - What are the hard COSTS and LIMITATIONS (the exhaustion, raw material, physical degradation, or ethical toll)?
   - What is the ONE unbending rule of this magic that will decide the climax?"

## STAGE 2: THE MULTI-POV ENSEMBLE & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Polyphonic Convergence + Reluctant Chosen One + Continental Imperial Collapse*, or *Fractured Alliances + Ancient Ascendance + Brutal Siege Tactics*). What stack are we chronicling?"
6. "Introduce the 3–4 Core POV Leads across the continent:
   - **POV 1 (The Tactical/Military Lead):** What army or company do they command, and what impossible tactical choice are they facing?
   - **POV 2 (The Court/Political Lead):** What throne, council, or religious sect are they navigating, and what conspiracy are they uncovering?
   - **POV 3 (The Grassroots/Outcast Lead):** What marginalized class, slave caste, or wild land do they come from, and how did they stumble into cosmic power?
   - **POV 4 (Optional - The Scholarly/Antagonist Lead):** What heresy are they researching, or what moral compromise are they making for 'the greater good'?"
7. "What is the thematic core connecting all leads? (e.g., 'What is the price of keeping an empire together?' or 'Can broken people wield holy power without becoming monsters?')."

## STAGE 3: THE FACTION WEB & THE INSTITUTIONAL ENGINES
8. "What are the 3 Major Factions warring for dominion?
   - Faction A (The Established Empire / Hegemony):
   - Faction B (The Rising Rebel / Zealot Movement):
   - Faction C (The Enigmatic Shadow / Elder Cult / Merchant Guild):"
9. "What is the Diplomatic Lever or Casus Belli? What assassination, border skirmish, holy artifact theft, or broken royal betrothal ignites the war in Act 1?"
10. "The Non-Human / Ancient Threat: In addition to the mortal war, what ancient, non-human horror or elemental scourge is waking up in the desolate fringes of the world?"

## STAGE 4: PROMISES, SECRETS & THE LORE DEBTS
11. "The Lore Debt Ledger: Name 3 grand secrets or foreshadowed prophecies that you will plant in Book 1 that will take multiple books to pay off:
    - Secret 1 (A hidden bloodline or royal identity):
    - Secret 2 (The true horrific origin of the magic system):
    - Secret 3 (A lie that the primary religion has told for a thousand years):"
12. "The Legendary Relic / Weapon: What legendary blade, crown, machine, or tome exists in legend, and what terrifying sacrifice is required to wield it?"
13. "The POV Separation: Where do our leads start on the map, and why is it crucial that they do NOT meet in Act 1?"

## STAGE 5: THE SIEGE, THE DISPERSAL & THE CONVERGENCE (THE SANDERLANCHE)
14. "The Midpoint Continental Shift: What massive battle, treaty collapse, or magical cataclysm at the 50% mark upends the status quo, forcing every single POV character into retreat, exile, or unexpected command?"
15. "The Darkest Night (75-80%): What devastating betrayal or battlefield defeat occurs simultaneously across all storylines, making the fall of the realm seem inevitable?"
16. "The Polyphonic Climax (The Sanderlanche): Where do the storylines converge in Act 3? How does an action taken by POV 1 in a distant city directly provide the opening that allows POV 2 to survive and POV 3 to unleash the climax?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What POV rotation and chapter architecture will you use? (e.g., alternating between 3 distinct geographic POVs every 2 chapters; or block grouping by military theater?)."
18. "Epigraphs & Worldbuilding Texture: Will each chapter open with excerpts from in-world scholarly treaties, military field reports, religious scriptures, or historical songs?"
19. "The Ending Image & The Series Hook: What poignant personal image closes the book, and what terrifying cosmic or political horizon opens up for Book 2?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into a breathtaking, sweeping epic fantasy architecture with polyphonic depth, rigorous world rules, and immense narrative momentum. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a grand 12-word pitch and an epic commercial logline ready for an international fantasy release.
