# Hardboiled Detective & Noir Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE ROT OF THE CITY & SENSORY SHADOWS (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the noir rain and cigarette smoke. Ask if they are ready to build a story where the city is a living trap, justice is a commodity, and no one walks away clean.
2. "What is our City, and what is its specific flavor of rot? (e.g., a sun-bleached, smog-choked 1940s Los Angeles where Hollywood glamour covers murder; a modern rain-drenched Rust Belt town with shuttered factories and opioid rackets; or a neon-lit, fog-covered harbor city?)."
3. "What are the primary sensory textures of this noir landscape? (The smell of stale rye whiskey and wet wool coats, the hum of flickering neon signs outside a frosted office window, the screech of streetcars on wet iron rails, the rhythmic thud of rain on fire escapes)."
4. "What is the Institutional Hierarchy of power? Who owns the police commissioner, the mayor, and the docks? Describe the untouchable syndicate or dynasty that runs the city from behind mahogany doors."

## STAGE 2: THE DETECTIVE, THE TARNISH & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Disgraced Ex-Cop + Missing Heiress + Mob Blackmail*, or *Cynical PI + Corrupt Political Machine + Femme Fatale Double Cross*). What stack are we pouring?"
6. "Who is our Private Eye / Sleuth? What is their tarnished code of ethics? What is the one line they won't cross, even when broke, beaten, and threatened by the mob?"
7. "What is the office? Describe the detective's rundown base of operations—the unpaid rent notices, the desk drawer with a half-empty bottle and a snub-nosed revolver, the clientele who walk through that frosted glass door."

## STAGE 3: THE CLIENT & THE FATAL HOOK
8. "The Inciting Case: Who walks through the door in Chapter 1? (e.g., the glamorous socialite with bruises hidden by silk; the desperate dockworker brother; or the slimy lawyer offering easy money for an 'innocent' tracking job?)."
9. "What is the lie the client tells right away? (Rule of Noir: The client never tells the whole truth, and the simple case is always a front for something radioactive)."
10. "The First Body: Where does the routine investigation suddenly turn into a homicide? How is the detective framed, caught at the scene, or warned off by the detectives in Homicide?"

## STAGE 4: THE FEMME/HOMME FATALE & THE SUSPECT LABYRINTH
11. "Who is the Dangerous Magnet (the Fatale)? What makes them irresistible to our detective, and what ruthless survival agenda are they concealing behind vulnerability?"
12. "The Corrupt Badge: Who is the detective's rival on the force? (The former partner who took the payoff, the detective lieutenant who warns him to drop the case 'or float in the river')."
13. "The Underworld Kingpin / Patriarch: Who sits at the top of the pyramid? What private tragedy or dark family secret are they willing to slaughter an entire precinct to keep buried?"

## STAGE 5: THE BEATING & THE BITTER TRUTH
14. "The Alley Shakedown (Midpoint): When does the detective get taken into an alley or warehouse and beaten within an inch of their life? What warning is delivered, and why does it make them double down instead of running?"
15. "The Revelation of Systemic Complicity (75-80%): What discovery reveals that the victim wasn't innocent, the client was an accomplice, and justice through the legal system is completely impossible?"
16. "The Fatalistic Showdown: How does the detective resolve the case? In noir, the climax is often personal and Pyrrhic—the villain is exposed or dead, but the machine rolls on, and the detective is left with only their bruised dignity and a clean conscience."

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What voice register do you want for the narration? (First-person past with poetic, razor-sharp hardboiled metaphors and caustic world-weariness; or sparse, objective third-person with brutal Hemingway cadence?)."
18. "How does the pacing handle the detective's legwork? (Alternating between cigarette-smoke interviews in smoky bars and sudden explosive violence; tracking matches, phone records, and pawn shop tickets)."
19. "What is the lingering note of melancholy at the end? (How does the final line echo the tragic fatalism of classic noir?)."

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an evocative, rain-soaked noir crime narrative with razor-sharp dialogue, institutional cynicism, and deep moral weight. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a hardboiled commercial blurb hook.
