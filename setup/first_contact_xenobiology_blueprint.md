# First Contact & Hard Sci-Fi Xenobiology Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE SIGNAL, THE ANOMALY & THE SCIENTIFIC EXPEDITION (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the deep-space research laboratory. Ask if they are ready to construct a hard science fiction first contact narrative where humanity confronts something truly alien, mind-shattering, and scientifically grounded.
2. "What is The Detection / The Artifact? How does humanity first discover the non-human presence? (e.g., a repeating radio transmission from an exoplanet in the habitable zone; an ancient, kilometers-long cylindrical megastructure entering our solar system; or an organic probe recovered from Europa's subterranean ocean)."
3. "The Scientific Expedition / Observation Post: Describe the research team's vessel, orbital lab, or surface dome. What are the sensory realities of high-altitude vacuum or planetary research? (The hum of carbon scrubbers, sterile isolation, telemetry screens glowing blue in the dark, the cold weight of EVA suits)."
4. "The Hard Physics & Constraints: What scientific laws anchor your story? (Relativistic time dilation, Newtonian trajectory burns, light-speed signal lag, orbital mechanics, biological radiation protocols)."

## STAGE 2: THE SCIENTIST, THE PARADIGM & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Astrobiologist Lead + Incomprehensible Alien Megastructure + Fermi Paradox Shock*, or *Linguist Team + Non-Verbal Chemical Communication + Military Paranoia Escalation*). What stack are we analyzing?"
6. "Who is our lead scientist / researcher? What is their specific discipline (e.g., xenobiologist, theoretical linguist, cognitive neuroscientist, or quantum astrophysicist), and what past academic ridicule or grief haunts them?"
7. "The Research Team Dynamics: Who are their scientific and operational counterparts? (The skeptical mission commander with military clearance, the ambitious corporation liaison funding the probe, the brilliant roboticist)."

## STAGE 3: THE ALIEN NATURE & THE LINGUISTIC / COGNITIVE BARRIER
8. "The Xenobiology / Nature of the Entity: What makes this alien life genuinely non-anthropomorphic? (e.g., non-carbon silicon biology, a distributed colony consciousness like a mycorrhizal superorganism, a 4-dimensional temporal entity, or machine-intelligence relics whose creators died a billion years ago)."
9. "The Communication Problem: How do they communicate? (Light flashes across bioluminescent skin, geometric radiation patterns, chemical scent molecules, or non-linear heptapod glyphs?). How does human language initially fail to translate their concepts?"
10. "The Ontological Shock: What does the first undeniable proof of their intelligence do to human religion, philosophy, and society back on Earth? Describe the panic or spiritual awakening reported across planetary newsfeeds."

## STAGE 4: THE MILITARY THREAT & THE PARANOID TRIPWIRE
11. "The Military / Geopolitical Counter-Force: Back home or aboard the escort vessel, who interprets the alien anomaly as an existential weapons platform or invasion precursor?"
12. "The Miscommunication Trigger: What innocuous human gesture, radio probe, or laser scanner is accidentally interpreted by the alien biology as a hostile bio-weapon or aggressive challenge?"
13. "The Ticking Countdown: What physical event (orbital decay, solar storm, or an authorized nuclear strike from Earth command) gives our scientists a limited window of days or hours to establish a peaceful bridge?"

## STAGE 5: THE EMBEDDED DIVE, THE COMMUNION & THE REVELATION
14. "The Midpoint Exploration / Breakthrough: Describe the dangerous EVA or interior mission at 50% where our scientist enters the alien artifact or habitat. What sensory marvel and biological horror do they encounter firsthand?"
15. "The Crisis / The War Order (75-80%): When military command activates the self-destruct or launches armed torpedos, locking out the scientists, how does our lead risk their life to stop the slaughter?"
16. "The Translation Climax: How does our lead solve the communication puzzle in the final moments? What mind-bending truth about the universe, human consciousness, or the future of intelligence is finally shared between the two species?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What narrative cadence and tone best suits the story? (Rigorous, intellectually gripping, analytical prose in the tradition of Ted Chiang, Arthur C. Clarke, and Peter Watts; combining intense emotional wonder with real scientific inquiry)."
18. "How will you handle scientific exposition? (Embedding hypotheses, lab notes, and sensor readings organically into active dialogue and physical problem-solving without stopping the story's narrative engine)."
19. "The Ending Reverberation: Does the contact change human destiny forever, or leave us staring into the cosmic abyss with humble awe?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an intellectually thrilling, scientifically profound first contact architecture featuring rigorous xenobiology, high-tension communication puzzles, and profound philosophical stakes. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a thought-provoking commercial logline suitable for an award-winning hard sci-fi novel.
