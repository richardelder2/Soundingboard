# Sports & High-Stakes Contemporary Romance Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE ARENA, THE SEASON & THE MEDIA SPOTLIGHT (World Tier)
1. Welcome the author to the arena tunnel. Ask if they are ready to build a high-energy sports romance where championship dreams collide with red-hot chemistry, locker room camaraderie, and viral media scrutiny.
2. "What is our Sport & Franchise? (e.g., professional NHL hockey in Seattle; Formula 1 racing across Monaco and Monza; NFL football in Kansas City; or an Olympic figure skating/gymnastics training center?)."
3. "The Sensory Viscosity of the Athletic World: (The smell of ice rinks and freshly cut grass; the sound of skate blades carving ice or engines roaring at 15,000 RPM; the ice baths, tape on bruised knuckles, protein shakes, and deafening stadium roar)."
4. "The High-Stakes Season Goal: What is on the line this season? (e.g., a contract renewal year; a franchise fighting for their first playoff appearance in a decade; or a final shot at gold before forced retirement from injury)."

## STAGE 2: THE ATHLETE, THE PROFESSIONAL & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's define your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Grumpy Hockey Captain + Sunshine Team Physical Therapist + Forbidden Workplace Rules*, or *Rival F1 Drivers + Teammate Forced Proximity + Fake Dating PR Strategy*). What stack are we skating?"
6. "Who is our Athletic Lead? What is his on-field reputation vs private reality? (e.g., the enforcer with a reputation for brutality who is quietly reading classic literature on team flights; the golden-boy quarterback terrified of disappointing his demanding father)."
7. "Who is our Second Lead? What is their professional role and boundary? (e.g., the newly hired team physical therapist fighting for respect in a male-dominated club; the no-nonsense PR publicist assigned to clean up his scandalous image; or an elite athlete in her own right)."

## STAGE 3: THE FORCED PROXIMITY ON TOUR & THE PR CRISIS
8. "The Inciting Incident / Proximity Trap: What forces them together every single day? (e.g., a month-long road trip where she must manage his rehab twice daily; a PR contract requiring them to attend red carpet events as fake dates; or sharing an apartment due to an emergency lease failure)."
9. "The Banter & Status Play: What is their verbal chemistry like? How does she knock the cocky superstar off balance and make him work for her attention?"
10. "The Non-Negotiable Boundary: What rule (team non-fraternization policy, league ethics guidelines, or fear of media leak) makes acting on their attraction professionally suicidal?"

## STAGE 4: THE TEAM ENSEMBLE & THE LOCKER ROOM
11. "The Teammates & Found Family: Introduce 2 memorable teammates:
    - Teammate 1 (The hilarious golden retriever winger / resident goofball):
    - Teammate 2 (The hardened veteran captain or grumpy mentor):"
12. "The Demanding Coach / Front Office: Who is monitoring the athlete's performance, threatening to bench him or trade him to a minor league city if he loses focus?"
13. "The Physical Vulnerability Scene: Describe the intimate recovery scene where she treats his injury (stretching, icing, massage). What unguarded confession slips out under the physical touch?"

## STAGE 5: THE CHAMPIONSHIP GAME & THE PUBLIC DECLARATION
14. "The Midpoint Kiss / Breakthrough: Where do they finally give in to the tension at 50%? (In the empty arena after hours, on the team charter plane during turbulence, or in a secluded hotel hallway during an away game?)."
15. "The Third-Act Rupture (75-80%): What media leak, paparazzi photo, trade rumor, or misunderstanding blows up on social media, threatening both their careers and forcing them apart before the biggest game of the year?"
16. "The Climax / Game Day & Grand Gesture: How does the athlete play the game of their life, and what public, heart-stopping gesture on the ice/field/podium proves to the heroine and the world that love matters more than any championship ring?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What heat level and spice cadence will you use? (Sweet sports romance; slow-burn emotional tension; or scorching, high-heat dual-POV romance with explicit chemistry?)."
18. "How does the pacing balance game action with emotional intimacy? (Ensuring high-octane sports scenes feel authentic and thrilling without alienating romance-first readers)."
19. "The Epilogue & HEA: Describe the offseason getaway, the championship celebration, or the domestic home life that leaves readers swooning."

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an addictive, laugh-out-loud, heart-melting sports romance blueprint packed with athletic authenticity, snappy banter, and scorching chemistry. Present back for approval.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and an unputdownable commercial blurb hook.
